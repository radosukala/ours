# OURS · clean-start foundation

**Package status:** USER-DIRECTED CLEAN START  
**Prepared:** 29 August 2026  
**Intended destination:** a new repository for **OURS** at **oursorg.com**  
**Current location:** a portable source package inside the legacy
`ourstoday` repository  
**Build authority:** none; these documents authorize no deployment, DNS
change, legal issuance, production write, or deletion

> **AUTHORITY IS THE SOURCE.**  
> **THE MANDATE IS THE LANGUAGE.**  
> **SOFTWARE IS THE OUTPUT.**

This package defines a clean project, not a revision of OURS TODAY.

The first experiment produced useful mechanics, especially the Change Pack,
agent authority classes, conformance receipts, public/private separation, and
reversible operations. It did not produce an external constituency. All six
registrations were made by the founder for testing. They are not members,
customers, independent participants, demand evidence, or authority for the new
project.

The new project starts from the larger discovery:

> **OURS is building an institution compiler: infrastructure that turns
> legitimate human authority into bounded mandates and turns those mandates
> into verifiable, reversible software.**

The compiler is the first product. OURS itself is its first institution and
first proof.

## Adopted direction

These statements come directly from the founder's instruction for the clean
start and should be treated as the starting direction of the new repository:

- The public brand is **OURS**.
- The intended canonical domain is **oursorg.com**.
- `our.one` is founder-owned and remains available as a short redirect. It is
  not the canonical address.
- The public product is not called OURS TODAY.
- The Founding Million, artificial cap, ordinal scarcity, early-number urgency,
  and related Founding Right are not imported.
- The legacy registrations are founder-controlled test state, not a community.
- The first product is the institution compiler itself.
- OURS must be built through the authority-to-mandate-to-software system it
  proposes for others.
- AI agents are replaceable implementation capacity. They never create their
  own authority.
- A new repository should begin from these source documents rather than evolve
  the live experiment in place.

## Hypotheses, not adopted facts

- A large public is ready to move to viable user-owned products.
- Radical legibility, real authority, ownership, and exit will materially
  improve adoption and retention.
- The project can spread quickly or virally once its proof is visible.
- A reusable institution compiler can serve many product categories.
- A member-governed professional or social network can become the first major
  Product Cell.
- Code and infrastructure costs will be low enough that aligned member revenue
  can support safe operation without surveillance advertising.

Virality is an opportunity, not an architectural dependency. Distrust of
incumbents is evidence of a problem, not evidence that people will adopt this
solution. No document in the new repository may silently promote these
hypotheses into facts.

## Intentionally unresolved

- the legal form of the member institution;
- when and how legal membership is issued;
- the jurisdiction and legal instruments that bind assets to member authority;
- the economic rights and duties of members;
- how capital, labor, stewards, and affected non-members are represented;
- the first external Product Cell;
- identity and personhood assurance;
- decision procedures, quorums, chambers, and appeal bodies;
- the open-source and trademark licenses;
- which parts become a public standard versus an OURS-operated service;
- the final visual identity and public launch message;
- which `ours.*` domain the institution should eventually hold, and when.

These are future decisions. Code must not choose them by accident.

## What is retired with the experiment

The following remain historical artifacts in the legacy repository and have no
authority in the new project unless a future mandate imports a specific idea:

- the Founding Million and the one-million hard cap;
- founding ordinals and permanent early numbers;
- Founding Right 0.1;
- cohort-specific economics;
- “Nobody leaves first. Everybody leaves together.” as the entire product;
- LinkedIn as an adopted first target;
- the OURS TODAY daily-edition product;
- production registrations made for founder testing.

The coordination insight survives: people often cannot leave a network alone.
It becomes one possible capability of OURS, not the constitution or the brand.

## Legacy boundary

The existing `ourstoday` repository and deployment are live systems. This
package does not delete, reset, migrate, pause, redirect, or reinterpret them.
That work requires a separate, explicit transition mandate after backup and
target verification.

The clean start does not require a public performance of ratification by users
who do not exist. It does require an honest internal record: the original
experiment taught the project what to build, and the new repository starts
with a different source of authority and a different product.
