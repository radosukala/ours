**Status:** SUPERSEDED — by `D-0006` on 8 September 2026; adopted by the founder on 4 September 2026, and nothing was built under it
**Class:** CHARTER
**Authority:** founder bootstrap
**Date drafted:** 2 September 2026

**Architecture adopted by this decision:**

## The decision

Charter a provisional Product Cell named **feed**, under founder bootstrap,
and build **OURS Feed v0**: a familiar social feed — profiles, posts,
follows, a home stream — in which every consequential behavior is a written
rule with a reason, every option in the settings carries the decision that
created it, and the defaults are set by decision rather than by whoever
profits from them.

Build it through the kernel. Release it, if at all, under a separate
mandate.

## Why

Three rounds of direction work established that mechanism is not the
attraction: people are not moved by how the machine works, but by what it
means for the products they use every day. The kernel, the trace, and the
refusal buy credibility with the few whose skepticism can end the project.
They give nobody a reason to arrive. A product does.

The founder asked whether a pilot should bypass the process to move faster.
It should not, and it does not need to: under founder bootstrap the entire
chain for this pilot is this decision and one mandate. What the chain costs
is an afternoon. What it buys is the product's only differentiator — a
settings page whose receipts are real. A pilot built outside the chain would
have to invent them.

## What this decision authorizes

| Mandate | Class | What it may do |
|---|---|---|
| `M-0003` | BUILD | build OURS Feed v0, its records, and the kernel checks; **may not deploy it** |
| `M-0003-RELEASE` | DEPLOY | publish one verified build to a named environment, with a named data controller; **not yet drafted** |

The separation is exercised, not asserted: the mandate that builds the
product cannot release it, and the release mandate cannot change what it
releases.

## The provisional charter

This is a bootstrap charter, held by the founder as sole steward, and it is
written to be replaced by one members adopt.

| Charter field | Bootstrap value |
|---|---|
| mission | a feed that takes orders from the person using it, not from an engagement objective |
| prohibited purposes | engagement maximisation, advertising, behavioral data sale, paid ranking, status competition |
| constituency | an invited cohort of adults; capped by what one steward can support, with no right or status attached to the cap |
| affected persons | people named or discussed in posts — standing is `DECLARED`, not implemented |
| steward and operator | the founder, alone |
| local decision classes | none delegated; every decision is founder bootstrap |
| financial model | none; no payment surface exists in v0 |
| data and algorithm contract | `rules/feed/` — rulebook, option catalog, ranking contract |
| appeal destination | **none during bootstrap** — recorded as a known gap |
| dissolution, export, fork | export and deletion from day one; the rules are repository records; a fork drill precedes cohort growth |

## Direction adopted

The founder adopts the architecture in `foundation/FEED-PILOT.md`, and in
particular:

- **one governed feed engine, one operating constituency first** —
  professional, because it scores best on `FIRST-PRODUCT.md` §15 and is the
  network the founder can invite; an image-first skin is configuration, not
  a second product;
- **three layers** — `FLOOR`, `GOVERNED`, `PERSONAL` — with the rule that
  where a behavior can be personal it is, and that withholding a feasible
  option requires a published reason;
- **defaults set by decision**, labelled in body type as set by founder
  bootstrap and not yet voted;
- **settings with receipts** — the settings page rendered from the option
  catalog, every option showing its rule, decision, and mandate, validated
  by the kernel so that an option without provenance refuses the build;
- **a ranking contract** whose prohibited signals are excluded by the
  ranking function's input type;
- **endorsement of defaults recorded as demand**, never rendered as a vote;
- **adults only, invite-only, minimum identity, no analytics, no
  advertising**;
- **export and deletion before any other feature is considered done**;
- **the reversal proof retargeted.** `FIRST-PRODUCT.md` §6 reserved `M-0003`
  for reversing the Authority Trace. That proof is more meaningful exercised
  on this pilot's first release, where a reversal affects people, and it is
  scheduled there. The Authority Trace stays as it is.

## What this decision does not authorize

- membership, ownership, or any legal interest for anyone;
- binding votes; endorsements are recorded demand only;
- points, ordinals, badges, ranks, tokens, or any scarce or transferable
  artifact;
- analytics, tracking, third-party scripts, or advertising;
- sale or ad-targeting use of behavioral data, or paid influence on ranking;
- accounts for, or features designed for, people under 18;
- any claim that members exist or that member ownership has been issued;
- production deployment under BUILD authority;
- naming a data controller as verified fact without a legal record;
- using a product-line name or domain that no decision has adopted.

## The counter-case

**A pilot can outrun its proof.** A working product with a settings page
that explains itself will be more persuasive than its evidence warrants —
and will be shown to people before the comprehension review, the legal
review, or the reversal proof has run. The predecessor project's error was
exactly this order.

Accepted knowingly, with structural mitigations rather than intentions: no
member claim can appear on any surface; every default carries its bootstrap
label in body type; provenance is rendered from records, not written into
templates; the release is a separate mandate that must name a data
controller and pass legal review of the privacy notice; and the reversal
proof runs on this pilot's first release.

**One steward holds every key.** Founder capture is not a hypothetical in
this Cell; it is the operating state. Every public surface of the pilot says
so, and the cohort cap exists because one person's honest capacity is small.

**Personal data exists for the first time.** OURS becomes a data controller
with legal obligations. This is the pilot's largest new responsibility, and
it is why nothing in `M-0003` may name the controller or invite anyone.

**Cold start.** A feed with nobody on it is a diary. The single-player floor
— a governed profile page, a settings page that explains itself, export —
is the hypothesis, and it is measured against real use, not assumed. If a
person alone finds nothing worth having, that is recorded as `FAILED`, not
reframed.

## Reasons

1. The demonstration funnel needs a surface people can touch, and every
   earlier artifact answers a question the public has not asked.
2. Building the pilot through the kernel is not slower than building it
   outside; it is the only way its receipts are real.
3. The settings page with provenance is a differentiator incumbents cannot
   copy without changing whom their products answer to.
4. The three-layer model keeps settings and governance apart, so that the
   pilot demonstrates sovereignty rather than a poll.
5. A professional constituency is the one that can be invited honestly and
   supported by one steward.
