## The decision

### A. The maintainer and the data controller

**Ctrl AI, Inc., the founder's company, is our.one's maintainer and its
data controller.** This settles D-0012 §A's `[CONFIRM]`.

**What public pages say about Ctrl AI.**

- They name it, and its role.
- Nothing more, because AGENTS.md §9 forbids facts about Ctrl AI that are
  not in verified legal records:
  - FOUNDING-AUTHORITY §4.1's registered office and agent are quoted from
    the certificate, but the filed record has never been retrieved;
  - so no address, agent, form or officer appears on a public page until it
    is.

**Still `[CONFIRM]`:**

- **The controller's contact email,** which `/privacy` and `/power` will
  show.
- **The Delaware entity record (file number),** if the address is to be
  published.
- **GDPR Article 27.** Ctrl AI is a US company serving people in the EU. It
  must either designate a representative in the EU, or count as established
  in the EU through the founder's work in Prague. Which of the two is a
  legal question. The release waits on a choice.
