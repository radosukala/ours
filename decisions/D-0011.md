## The decision

### A. Direction

OURS's first product is **a home for friends and the people you choose to
follow**:

- friendship is mutual; following is one-way;
- you see their posts in order, and the feed ends when you are caught up;
- the first act is one person inviting another: *Connect with me on OURS.*

The connections are the first asset the layer holds (`THE-LAYER.md` §4:
identity and relationships). Everything added later is an application on
them, and applications come and go.

### B. What it promises, and how it says so

**Working copy for the v0 application** (the naming decision is `D-0004`).
It passes the claims scan before any use:

> **OURS** · Stay connected. On our terms.
> A home for friends and people you choose to follow. Their posts, in
> order, with an end when you're caught up.
> We're building OURS so our connections can stay with us as apps
> change — and the people using it can control its future.
> Open code. Public costs. Clear rules.
> Connect with me on OURS.

**The status line, wherever control is described:** *Founder-led and
founder-funded at launch. Working toward control by the people using it.*

### C. Transparent from the first day

1. **Code.** The application's source is public under Apache-2.0, the
   licence the founder chose for oursnow on 10 September 2026. The running
   version is identified on the application's own pages.
2. **Money.** A public ledger separates:
   - contributions received;
   - costs paid, with redacted evidence;
   - what remains;
   - bills and commitments coming next.

   A pledge is a *commitment* until received. A figure is an *estimate*
   until invoiced. Whether founder money is repayable is stated.
   Provider credits are shown as credits. The founder's unpaid work is
   acknowledged without a valuation. The OURS ledger is kept separate from
   any other project of the same company. Contributions are asked for as
   *"Support OURS"*, never as tax-deductible unless the recipient qualifies,
   and they buy no reach and no say.
3. **Power.** A public page states who controls:
   - the domain;
   - hosting, the database and email sending;
   - releases;
   - moderation;
   - funds;
   - the code.

   A row with no recorded fact says *not yet recorded*.

### D. How it is built

1. **One core, one first-party application.** Mobile-ready web first. iOS
   and Android follow under their own mandates, on the same core.
2. **The foundation includes safety from the start:** blocking, muting,
   reporting, a person who acts on reports, removal with a statement of
   reasons, export and deletion.
3. **Minimal data:**
   - no contact upload;
   - no search and no "people you may know";
   - no public graph;
   - no third-party code touching the graph;
   - no ranking;
   - no advertising;
   - no photos until reporting and removal are proven;
   - adults only in v0.
4. **Invite-only growth,** with a limit per person. Every account is
   invited by a person.
5. **One weekly email**, which says who posted and not what.
6. **The design is familiar:** a system font stack and the layout people
   know from the social apps they use.

### F. The operator and the holder

- **The starting operator.** Ctrl AI, Inc. (Delaware) is the founder's
  *proposed* starting operator. Its authority, asset holdings and
  responsibility for OURS are `[CONFIRM]` and are not published as fact
  until recorded. A later conversion to a public benefit corporation is
  possible. Neither the company nor a conversion is the ownership mechanism.
- **The ownership arrangement is `D-0007`'s question,** and it stays DRAFT.
  It must answer who can change the rules, appoint operators, sell
  essential assets, and authorize continuity if the founder leaves.
- **Release waits on `D-0007`.** No real person's data is held under this
  decision until `D-0007`'s conditions that apply to this application are
  adopted, and until a release mandate names the environment and the
  controller.

## Prohibited under this decision

- *"Not for sale"*, *"user-owned"*, *"member-owned"*, *"co-owner"*, or any
  claim of ownership, in the present tense;
- any figure on the ledger that is not a recorded fact, a stated
  commitment, or a labelled estimate;
- claims of tax deductibility;
- deploying anything under `M-0010`;
- holding any real person's data before `D-0007`'s conditions that apply to
  this application and a release mandate;
- third-party applications, ranking, advertising, contact upload, search,
  or a public graph in v0.
