## The decision

### A. The first screen

In this order, for a visitor:

1. the headline, unchanged (D-0020 §A);
2. one line: *"It starts with a friends feed. Building toward much more."*
   (agent's choice: the notes quote "It starts with a friends feed…" and
   nothing after it; these are the last two sentences of the front door's
   existing description, already held to D-0020 §F);
3. the count against the threshold, as *"N of 100,000 people are in."*, and
   under it the founder's pledge, as a quotation signed *"Rado,
   maintainer"*: *"When 100,000 people have joined, I hand over its domain,
   its data and the right to replace me to a not-for-profit body of its
   members. Until then, I hold all three."* (agent's choice of which
   sentence "your pledge" means: the notes' "your pledge" is read as the
   signed promise /feed already carries, D-0016 §C, word for word. The
   claims scan lists every sentence about the handover by exact text and
   file, so the front door's file gets its own entry for that one sentence,
   as the repository's own rule asks, and no other);
4. the status line, *"Founder-led today. User control isn't built yet."*,
   with its link (D-0020 §F, unchanged);
5. **one form**: the address, and the optional question *"Which app would you
   take back?"*, under the button's words (D-0016 §B, unchanged) and the
   lines under it.

The two entrance buttons leave the first screen (agent's choice: the notes
say "one form", and two buttons that scroll the page are not a second way
in). Where they led stays: the header's four places, the builders' section
and *your part* are unchanged.

For a member (D-0023 §C), the form's place holds *"You're in."* and *"Open
your feed"*, as everywhere a visitor is asked to join. The count is the same
line, with no rank.

There is one form on the page. The feed's panel under *A first project* no
longer carries a second one; it points to the first.

### B. A named app

- **What is kept:** the words, and the day they were named (UTC). Nothing
  else: no address, no account, no network address, no browser detail, and no
  key that could join it to any of them. The day, not the moment, is
  deliberate (agent's choice: the notes say "words and time only"): the
  moment would let anyone who can read the database match a name to the
  waiting list by its time.
- **How long it can be:** 140 characters (agent's choice). A longer one is
  refused at the field, in words.
- **When it is kept:** only when the request is a valid one, after the same
  two rate limits a seat request has. A request that is refused, because
  joining is off, the address isn't one or a limit holds, keeps nothing. A
  need that can't be stored (the table isn't there yet, the database is
  unwell) never refuses the seat: it is logged by name, and the person gets
  the one answer, as before.
- **The answer is the one answer** (D-0015 §H). It says nothing about the
  need, the address, or what either did.
- **Who reads it, and where:** the founder, in the database. Nothing
  publishes it, groups it, ranks it, or sends it anywhere.
- **What it can't be:** a request to delete. Nothing in a stored need says
  whose it is, so it can't be found to be deleted, and /privacy says so. It
  asks people not to put anything about themselves in it.

### C. The count of them

A line under the form while at least one has been named: *"1 app named so
far."*, *"12 apps named so far."* It is the number stored. It counts what
people typed, not different apps: five people can name one, and the page
doesn't claim they named five. (Whether it should say "answers" is open to
the founder.) A count that can't be read leaves the line out.

### D. The member's panel

The panel beside the feed (D-0023 §D) carries, under its text, *"N of
100,000 people are in."*, the same number as the first screen's. When the
count can't be read, the line is left out; the panel and the feed never wait
on it.

### E. The link card

- The root of every public page carries a card: the title, the description,
  the address, and an image of the headline on the paper, 1200 × 630, which
  X, Facebook, Slack and anything that reads a page's card show.
- It reads nothing from the database. The base of its addresses is the
  site's own, `APP_URL`; if that is missing, the card is left without a base
  rather than any page failing.
- The image is made by `scripts/card.ts`, by hand, from the headline's own
  words, in the site's own colours, and committed. Nothing is fetched to make
  it or to show it.
- What can't be changed from here: X, Facebook and Slack keep the card they
  already hold until they fetch the link again.

### F. The records

`SPEC.md` §18.23 says all of this. The kit's manifest (`our.one.json`) and
`/privacy` each gain an entry for what a named app keeps, **the same words in
both**, and the kit's own check compares them.

## What it does not decide

- **Publishing the named apps,** or doing anything else with them. Open, and
  the founder's.
- **How many seats to open** when this goes out. Open, and the founder's.
- **A deploy.** BUILD does not become DEPLOY (AGENTS.md §8). M-0021 is a
  BUILD mandate. A push to `main` is a deploy on this project; M-0021 names
  the founder's go for it.
- **Anything a named app might mean.** It is not a vote, a request, a
  commission or a promise.

## Prohibitions

- an address, an account, a network address or any other key stored with, or
  able to find, a named app;
- any page that shows, quotes, lists or links a named app;
- any sentence saying a named app will be built, is wanted by a number of
  people, or gives anyone a say;
- using the count of them as authority, or as evidence of demand to a
  builder, until the founder decides it may be;
- a public page that depends on the database being up, or on a named-app
  count being readable;
- any change to what the feed does or to its verified words.
