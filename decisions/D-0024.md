## The decision

### A. The first screen says the whole thing

- **In this order, and nothing else:** the headline; what it is; the
  promise, with the count against its threshold; the status; one form.
- **What it is,** one line, the video's own: *"It starts with a friends
  feed: your people, newest first, and then it ends. No ads."* It replaces
  D-0020 §A's lede on the front door; `/feed` keeps its verified words.
- **The promise** is the pledge's sentence, as the contract and `/feed`
  already have it, with the count before it (§B).
- **The status line** stays: *"Founder-led today. User control isn't built
  yet."* with its link to where it stands.
- **The rest of the page stays** for people who read: the idea, the
  projects, what "ours" means, building, what's in the open, your part.
- *Interpretation:* the founder asked whether people would read so much.
  This record reads that as an order of things, not a cut.

### B. The count, against its threshold

- **A number, never a bar:** *"1 of 100,000"*, then the pledge's sentence.
  The count is the public count, accounts that exist and aren't suspended
  (D-0012 §B); the waiting list never counts. The threshold is the
  contract's.
- **Where:** on the first screen, and in the panel beside a member's feed.
- **When the count can't be read,** the sentence stands alone, as the count
  line does today.
- **A first wave of seats is opened when the post goes out,** by the
  founder, in the administrator's controls that exist. Its size is the
  founder's, still open. Without it the count can't move, and a count
  beside a closed door is worse than none.

### C. One form, and a need named

- **Two fields:** the address, and an optional line, *"Which app would you
  take back?"* One button, with D-0016 §B's words: *Join our.one* while a
  seat is open, *Join the waiting list* when none is. The seat line and
  the free line under it are unchanged. *I want this to exist* goes; the
  form is the entrance. *I want to build* stays, as a text link to
  `/build`.
- **A need named is kept** in a table of its own, without the address: its
  words, and when. At most 140 characters. It rides on the seat request's
  gates and rate limits, in the same submit, and the request goes through
  with or without it.
- **Counted in public:** *"37 apps named so far."* beside the form, from
  the first one. Read by the founder. **Not published.** Publishing them,
  and moderating them, is a later decision.
- **Kept for 12 months,** or until a decision publishes or deletes them.
  *Interpretation:* the founder named no time; 12 months is the draft's.
- **The privacy notice** says what is kept, why, and for how long, before
  the form takes anything.
- **The drafts stay** as D-0020 §E has them: in the browser, nothing saved,
  sent or counted. With `PROPOSALS_EMAIL` set, they can be sent; setting
  it is the founder's action, no code.

### D. A link card

- **Every public page** carries a title, a description and an image for
  link cards: the headline on the paper, in the identity. The image is a
  file in the repository, so what is shown is what was committed.

### E. Authorized

- **`M-0021` (BUILD):** §A to §D in `apps/web`.
- **Nothing is pushed** until the founder has seen the first screen's words
  and screenshots and said go (D-0023 §E's rule, again).

## Prohibited under this decision

- any sentence on the first screen that claims user control, ownership, a
  holder or a safeguard exists today (D-0020 §F);
- a progress bar, or any count that includes the waiting list;
- keeping a need with its address, or publishing a need, under M-0021;
- changing `/feed`'s verified words, the contract, or what the feed does.

## Reasons

1. **People from a post give the site ten seconds.** The video says three
   things; the first screen should say the same three, in the same order.
2. **One door, not twelve.** A visitor who wants to act should find one
   thing to do, and it should work.
3. **The number is the proposition.** "1 of 100,000" is honest, and it is
   the story; a bar would hide it.
4. **Demand is the first step** of the framework (D-0017): a need named is
   where a project starts.
