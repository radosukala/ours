## The decision

### A. One identity

- **Every page, signed in or not, has our.one's own look:** the paper, the
  ink, the rust and the acid, the wordmark with its rust dot, and the type
  of the public pages.
- **The feed keeps a familiar shape:** write at the top, then the posts,
  newest first, and the end. It doesn't keep X's colours, its left
  navigation or its black "Post" pill.
- **The pictures of the app on `/feed`** show its real look.
- *Interpretation:* D-0011 and P-0007 asked for a feed that looks familiar.
  The founder now finds that it copies X; familiar is kept, the copy is
  not.

### B. One header

- **Members get the header every visitor gets:** the wordmark, which goes
  to the front door, and the four places (the idea, projects, building,
  what's in the open).
- **On its right,** where a visitor has *Sign in*, a member has their feed,
  their notifications and their own pages.
- **On a phone,** the app keeps its bottom bar for the feed, people, writing,
  notifications and the profile.

### C. The front door is for members too

- **`/` no longer sends a member to `/home`.** The message and the mission
  are there for everyone.
- **Where a visitor is asked to join** (the feed's panel on the front door,
  and `/feed`), a member is shown their feed instead.
- *Interpretation:* after signing in, a member still lands on the feed:
  people sign in to use it, and the header shows the rest. The founder can
  change this.

### D. The feed, as the first project

- **In the app, the feed is called the feed,** not "Home".
- **Beside it, a panel says what our.one is:** the line, that the feed is
  its first project, and the ways to name a need or bring an idea, as the
  front door has them.

### E. Authorized

- **`M-0020` (BUILD):** §A to §D in `apps/web`.
- **Nothing is pushed** until the founder has seen screenshots and said go
  (D-0021 §J, D-0022 §D).

## Prohibited under this decision

- X's colours, its left navigation or its "Post" pill;
- a signed-in page with none of the network's places;
- sending a member away from the front door;
- changing what the feed does, or the feed's verified words, under
  M-0020: only how it looks and what surrounds it.

## Reasons

1. **our.one is the network, and the feed its first project** (D-0017,
   D-0020). A member who sees only the feed sees a feed app.
2. **One identity** is what the founder asked for, and what the records
   already gave the public pages.
