# D-0009 — Five class lines say what holds

**Status:** DRAFT — awaiting founder adoption
**Class:** CONSTITUTIONAL — an amendment under `R-AMENDMENT`, which requires
a decision naming the articles changed and the reason, and a receipt
**Authority:** founder bootstrap
**Proposal:** [`P-0006`](../proposals/P-0006.md)
**Date drafted:** 8 September 2026
**Machine projection:** [`D-0009.yaml`](./D-0009.yaml)

## The decision

Amend the class line of five articles of `constitution/CONSTITUTION-0.1.md`
to state what actually holds them, as of the kernel at `M-0006`. The
article bodies are unchanged. The reason, for each, is the difference
between the line and the code, recorded in `P-0006`.

Applied in the adopting commit, exactly:

**`R-SOURCE-HIERARCHY`**

> **Class:** `ENFORCED` for the ordering of recorded sources and for
> prerequisite decisions (`R-SOURCE-HIERARCHY/prerequisite`, since
> `M-0004`) · `INTERPRETED` for whether a given conflict is material

**`R-TRUTHFUL-STATUS`**

> **Class:** `ENFORCED` for the enumeration, and for adoption — a `DRAFT`
> or `PROPOSED` record in a chain authorises no execution
> (`R-TRUTHFUL-STATUS/adoption`, since `M-0004`) · `DECLARED` for whether a
> state beyond adoption matches the evidence, which no check decides ·
> `INTERPRETED` for `OBSERVED`

**`R-PUBLIC-PRIVATE`**

> **Class:** `STRUCTURAL` — a community's register of members is private by
> placement, never in the repository, and a specification's contract lists
> the fields an application may reach · `ENFORCED` at the gate, for
> applications, since `M-0006` — a disclosure of an undeclared field is
> refused (`S-CONTRACT-DECLARED`) · `DECLARED` for a projection check in the
> kernel and for a scan of receipts for secret-shaped strings, neither of
> which exists

**`R-EXACT-ARTIFACT`**

> **Class:** `DECLARED` — no release gate exists in Kernel 0.1; the one
> release so far was compared by a person, once, and receipted as `CHECKED`
> (`receipts/releases/2026-08-30-M-0001-RELEASE.md`) · `DECLARED` for
> attestation that the running artifact is that digest, which requires
> production infrastructure Kernel 0.1 does not have

**`R-NO-FICTIONAL-OWNERSHIP`**

> **Class:** `CHECKED` · `kernel: prohibited-claim scan over public text` —
> a pattern scan that cannot read polarity, which is why it reports rather
> than blocks; it flagged a denial as an assertion on its first run

## What this decision authorizes

| Mandate | Class | What it may do |
|---|---|---|
| `M-0008` | BUILD | a conformance test that fails when an article's class line names a kernel rule that no finding carries; an `ours articles` report printing each article's declared class beside the kernel's actual class; `apps/proof` importing the kernel's article parser instead of its own copy |

## What this decision does not authorize

- changing any article's body, or adding or removing an article;
- building the checks the corrected lines say do not exist — those are
  later mandates, if wanted;
- describing any of the five articles as held more strongly than the new
  lines say.

## The counter-case

**Relabelling downward reads as retreat.** It is the opposite: the
document promised that a class is a fact, and three lines were not. Two
of the five move upward. The receipt of this amendment is the record that
the constitution corrected itself when its own report caught it — which is
the behaviour `LIFECYCLE.md` §3 station 14 exists for.

**A test for class lines is machinery.** Small machinery for a real loop:
this drift happened, was found by a person writing a report nine days
late, and would have been found by the test the day the code changed.

## Reasons

1. A class is a fact about this repository, and three were false.
2. Two articles are held more strongly than written, and understatement is
   the same error.
3. The next drift should be caught by a machine.
