> this project to launc

**How this record was made.** An agent drafted it from those words. Where
it interprets them, it says so (§C, §D). The founder's review of the exact

**Class:** CONSTITUTIONAL. It settles D-0017 §K.2, publishes D-0017's

**Authority:** founder bootstrap
**Prerequisites:** `D-0016`, `D-0017`
**Machine projection:** [`D-0018.yaml`](./D-0018.yaml)

## The decision

### A. The seven safeguards are the rule

**D-0017 §I's seven safeguards are adopted** as the rule for every protected
service:

- the law;
- the agreement;
- no keys;
- reach;
- leave;
- the record;
- custody.

**Each safeguard's status is shown wherever it is described.** Today none
is built.

**No protected service except the feed touches anyone's data** before all
seven exist for it. The feed is D-0017 §D's stated exception: the founder
holds it until the holder exists.

This settles D-0017 §K.2.

### B. The common agreement

**The common agreement is drafted and published as `/agreement`.**

- It is marked *"being developed"*: none of its collective rights is in
  force yet.
- Each part says what holds it today.
- It carries D-0017's substance: the definition of "owned" (§B), what users
  and maintainers each get (§C), the two kinds of project (§D), traction and
  the holder (§E), money (§F), how a service starts (§G), and the data line
  with its safeguards (§I and §A above).

**The founder's review of its exact wording is PENDING.**

- The draft's source is in the public repository, marked *"being
  developed"*, like every draft record.
- It reaches the live site only after the founder's approval, which is a
  precondition of M-0012.

### C. The framework pages

The founder said *"continue to build this project to launc"*. **This record
reads that as building what D-0017 §H says the launch carries:**

- **`/agreement`:** the common agreement.
- **`/projects`:**
  - the feed as the first project, with its maintainer, its pay (none, by
    choice), its budget, what is held today and what is promised;
  - an invitation to propose the next one.
- **`/maintainers`, titled "Build the next one":**
  - what a maintainer does;
  - what they get and what they give up;
  - how to propose a service;
  - how anyone can name a need.

  Proposals and needs come **by email and are read by hand**. No form, no
  database and no marketplace software (D-0017 §H).
- **The site footer** links to the agreement, the projects and the
  maintainers page.
- **`/contract`** says that the feed will also run under the common
  agreement.
- **`/privacy`** says what happens to a proposal or a need sent by email.

**The front page's headline and lede stay as they are** until the five-stranger
test is run (D-0017 §H). Its footer gains the links, like every other
page's.

### D. Where proposals go

**The address is the founder's choice:** a new setting, `PROPOSALS_EMAIL`
`[CONFIRM]`.

- **While the setting is empty,** the pages say proposals open at launch,
  and show no address. A missing human decision switches the feature off;
  it is never defaulted.
- **This record reads** the founder's *"privacy@ctrlai.com, info@ and
  othhers"* (D-0014) as meaning more addresses exist. It does not choose
  one.

### E. The claims scan

These sentences are let through by exact text, **on `/agreement` only**, as
D-0017 §J requires:

- the definition of "owned";
- the sentence calling a service owned only when all of it holds;
- the agreement's denial of investment, in promise 1's form.

Anywhere else, the scan still refuses them.

### F. Authorized

- **`M-0015` (BUILD):** the framework pages of §B to §E, in `apps/web`.
- **`M-0012` amended (it is a draft).** Two preconditions are added:
  - the founder's approval of the agreement's exact wording;
  - `PROPOSALS_EMAIL` set to the address the founder chooses.

## Prohibited under this decision

- **Describing any safeguard as built** before it is.
- **Changing the front page's headline or lede** before the message test.
- **Collecting proposals or needs by any route other than email** under
  M-0015.
- **Putting the agreement on the live site** before the founder approves its
  wording.

## Reasons

1. **The founder asked for a line maintainers can't cross.**
   - The safeguards are THE-LAYER §6–§7.1's design, with each class stated.
   - Showing every one as not built keeps the line honest until it is built.
2. **The framework has to be readable by launch day,** and builders need a
   way in that works without software. Email, read by hand, is the least
   machinery for one real loop (AGENTS.md §12).
3. **The front page's message waits for evidence,** as D-0017 §H decided.
