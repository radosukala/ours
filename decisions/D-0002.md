# D-0002 — Record the operator, and close the scope gap

**Status:** DRAFT — awaiting founder adoption
**Class:** CONSTITUTIONAL
**Authority:** founder bootstrap
**Date drafted:** 30 August 2026
**Machine projection:** [`D-0002.yaml`](./D-0002.yaml)

## The decision

Two corrections at the authority layer, taken together because both concern
whether this repository's records say what is true.

1. Record the transitional operator's entity facts, as stated by the founder
   and marked unverified, in `authority/FOUNDING-AUTHORITY.md`.
2. Close the gap that let an implementation write to a path its mandate
   denied, without any check objecting.

## Part one — the operator

On 30 August 2026 the founder stated:

```text
ENTITY              Ctrl AI, Inc.
FORM                Delaware C-Corporation
REGISTERED OFFICE   800 North King Street, Wilmington, DE 19801
```

This is recorded as **stated by the founder, not verified against a filing.**
`AGENTS.md` §9 prohibits asserting facts about Ctrl AI, Inc. that are not
present in verified legal records, and a founder's statement in conversation
is not such a record. The distinction is kept in the document itself.

Two things this does not establish, noted so the record is not read as more
than it is:

- **A Delaware registered office is not a place of business.** 800 North King
  Street is a commercial building offering registered-agent and virtual-office
  services. The address establishes the state of incorporation and where
  service of process is accepted. It says nothing about where the company
  operates, who directs it, or what it holds.
- **No relationship to this project is established.** Whether Ctrl AI, Inc.
  owns the repository, the domain, or anything else remains open, and the
  Asset and Authority Map still records those as unresolved.

## Part two — the scope gap

While building `M-0001`, the implementation wrote
`receipts/builds/2026-08-30-M-0001.md`. `M-0001` denies `receipts/**`.

Two distinct failures:

**The mandate contradicted itself.** Its evidence section requires a build
receipt while its scope denies the only directory one can be written to. A
mandate that demands an artifact it forbids is not a bound; it is a trap, and
it guarantees either a violation or a missing receipt.

**Nothing objected.** `R-SCOPE` is classed `ENFORCED`, but it only evaluates
paths the caller supplies, and no caller supplied any. The trace reported,
accurately, *"Scope is coherent. No changed paths were supplied, so nothing
was checked against it"* — and that sentence was read as a pass.

So the article was enforced in the kernel and unenforced in practice. The
honest description of `R-SCOPE` until now is `DECLARED`, not `ENFORCED`.

This decision therefore authorizes:

- allowing `receipts/builds/**` in any mandate that requires a build receipt,
  so the contradiction cannot recur;
- wiring the scope check to the working tree, so `changedPaths` comes from
  what actually changed rather than from nothing;
- reporting `R-SCOPE` as `NOT_MACHINE_DECIDABLE` when no paths were supplied,
  rather than as a pass, so an unrun check never again reads as a clean one.

## Why this is not a quiet edit

The founder stated a fact in a conversation. `AGENTS.md` §4 says a
conversation is not a mandate, and `M-0001` denies `authority/**`. Editing the
root authority document because a human mentioned something in chat is the
precise failure this project exists to prevent, and the fact that the human is
the root authority does not change the procedure — it only means the decision
is available to be made.

So the fact is recorded through a decision that can be read, dated, and
disagreed with, rather than appearing in the document with no history.

## What this decision does not authorize

- asserting any Ctrl AI, Inc. fact as verified;
- recording ownership of any asset by any party;
- changing any constitutional article's text;
- deployment of anything.

## The counter-case

**Ceremony has a cost.** A three-line fact now carries a decision, a mandate,
an amendment, and a receipt. At scale this is how governance becomes theatre —
volume of process substituting for quality of judgment, which
`INSTITUTION-COMPILER.md` §20 names as "safety theater".

Accepted for now, because the alternative was demonstrated an hour ago: an
implementation wrote outside its scope and no mechanism noticed. Until the
mechanism works, the procedure is what is holding, and a bootstrap project
with one participant can afford the overhead. It should be revisited before
the second person joins, and the honest measure is whether receipts grow
faster than the evidence in them.
