## The decision

### A. North America

- **The functions run in Cleveland (`cle1`),** beside the database at Neon
  in Ohio (us-east-2). Both are in North America, side by side.
- **This replaces** D-0021 §D's Frankfurt and M-0012's "a database in an EU
  region".
- **The site says where it runs,** as D-0021 §C has it: on the deployed
  site, `/privacy` and `/power` name Cleveland and Ohio, from the server's
  own settings.

### B. What it costs, said

- **People in Europe wait longer for each page,** which comes from North
  America.
- **Their data is stored in the United States.** `/privacy` says where, and
  the legal basis for that transfer is a question for the legal review of
  `/privacy` (D-0012 §E, D-0021 §H). Until then, the notice names the place
  and nothing more about it.

### C. Authorized

- **`M-0019` (BUILD):** the region in `apps/web/vercel.json`, Cleveland's
  name on the pages, the feed's manifest line on where its data is kept,
  the spec and the tests.
- **`M-0012` amended (it is a draft):** the database in Ohio (us-east-2),
  the functions in Cleveland.

### D. The go

- **What the founder's "go" covers:** pushing `main`, which Vercel builds
  as production, once M-0019 is built and checked.
- **The database step** still runs only in the build of the commit the
  founder names in `OURS_RELEASE` (D-0021 §D).
- **M-0012 stays a draft:** its preconditions are not all met. The release
  receipt says which.

## Prohibited under this decision

- a page that names a region its server doesn't run in;
- a sentence that says the data is kept in the EU;
- deploying under `M-0019` by anything but the founder's go.

## Reasons

1. **The founder expects most people from outside Europe,** and no law
   requires data to be stored in the EU.
2. **The functions and the database belong together.** Cleveland sits
   beside the database that already exists.
