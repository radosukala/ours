## Why this decision comes before any person's data

A holder establishes custody. Ownership is established by what a person can
actually do. A colleague's review put it exactly: without this decision,
the fixture can pass the replacement test while the real service remains
founder-controlled hosting with an additional custodian. So before the
first real person's data exists on the layer, and before any community's
name is held, this decision states the rights people receive, how each is
exercised, who can remove an operator, what happens if a holder refuses,
and which powers remain with whom — and only then chooses a holding
arrangement that serves those rights.

## The decision

### A. The rights people receive at opening

Each right names who holds it, how it is exercised, and the class it is
held at when the layer opens. A row's class is a fact to be checked against
the running layer, not a promise.

| Right | Who holds it | How it is exercised | Class at opening |
|---|---|---|---|
| to leave with your data | every person | one action; a bundle the verifier accepts | `ENFORCED` |
| to see who decided anything that affects you | every person | the record, one tap from the surface | `STRUCTURAL` |
| to propose, and to be heard | every member with standing | the ring, by the community's charter | `STRUCTURAL` |
| to decide, by the charter's procedure | members with standing | a vote whose tally the gate checks; threshold `[CONFIRM — the first charter's]` | `ENFORCED` for the tally |
| to remove the operator | members, by threshold `[CONFIRM]` | a decision; the handover the records make possible; **rehearsed before opening** | `ORGANIZATIONAL SEPARATION` once rehearsed · `DECLARED` before |
| to replace the application and its supplier | the community | the replacement test of `THE-LAYER.md` §8, rehearsed on the fixture before opening | `DECLARED` until rehearsed on the real tool |
| to take everything if the holder refuses | the community | the fork drill from the community's slice; the holding agreement's hand-over duty; see §C for what is lost meanwhile | `DECLARED` · legal control absent |
| to challenge a decision | anyone affected | before it, the discussion; after it, reversal on request; appeal outside the community: **none during bootstrap, recorded as a gap** | `INTERPRETED` · appeal `DECLARED` |

### B. Removing an operator

Who may call it: `[CONFIRM — e.g. any five members]`. Who decides: members,
by `[CONFIRM — threshold]`. What follows: the handover procedure the
records make possible — a second person runs the layer's operations for the
community from the records alone — **rehearsed once before the layer opens,
with the rehearsal's receipt naming what broke.** An operator who cannot be
removed is a proprietor with a title.

### C. If the holder refuses

The holding agreement obliges the holder to hand over the community's
assets — name, deploy key, fiscal account — on the community's own
procedure, and forbids acquiring anything. If the holder refuses anyway:
the community forks from its slice — records, data, specification — under
another operator, with everything except the assets the holder physically
controls. **What is lost meanwhile, stated rather than implied:** the
address, until a legal form makes the agreement enforceable; the money in
the fiscal account, until the fiscal host's own dispute process resolves it
`[CONFIRM — the host's terms]`. Class of the hand-over duty: `DECLARED` —
a written expectation with no mechanism yet — and said so.

### D. Residual powers

Named rather than implied; each a visible, receipted event when used.

| Who | Can still | Class |
|---|---|---|
| the founder | remove any check in this repository; change the layer's code before a second operator exists; decide alone during bootstrap | `AUTOMATED GATE` beneath one person — `R-WEAKEST-LAYER` |
| the operator | read everything inside the runtime boundary; pause the layer; restore a backup | `INTERPRETED` — receipted; reviewed by the community |
| the holder | refuse (§C); lose the accounts | `DECLARED` |

### E. The holding arrangement

Chosen among options, each evaluated against the four questions —
replacement, recovery, member rights, residual powers — with the founder's
choice marked `[CONFIRM]`:

| Option | Replacement | Recovery from the holder's failure | Member rights served | Residual powers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · one second-human holder** for registrar, deploy key, and fiscal account | the community can replace the operator; replacing the holder needs the holder | one person's absence blocks all three | custody only; no right in §A depends on it | the holder can refuse everything at once |
| **2 · split holders by asset class** — registrar with one person, deploy key with another, money at a fiscal host | as 1 | one failure blocks one asset | as 1 | no single refusal takes everything |
| **3 · fiscal host for money; founder keeps registrar and deploy key under a written assignment** | as 1 | money recoverable through the host; name and key not | as 1 | the founder's residual powers unchanged — honest, and weakest |
| **4 · a purpose-bound entity** | the entity's own procedure | its governing documents | can carry rights, not only custody | depends on its form; needs legal advice and time |

Founder's choice: `[CONFIRM]`. Whichever is chosen is bound by the transfer
gates of `FOUNDING-AUTHORITY.md` §8 and the unconditional exit of
`THE-LAYER.md` §10; and none of them is presented as user ownership.
Options 1–3 raise custody to `ORGANIZATIONAL SEPARATION` at best and remove
no operator risk; only option 4 can carry rights, and it is not available
today.

### F. Before opening

- a legal review of the privacy notice, and a named controller — or the gap
  stated on every surface `[CONFIRM]`;
- the operator-removal rehearsal of §B, receipted;
- the replacement test rehearsed on the fixture, receipted;
- every row of §A rendered on the layer's surface with its class, so that
  a reader can see which are held and which are written.

## What this decision does not authorize

- any mandate; it authorizes nothing and gates two things;
- any claim that the arrangement chosen in §E is ownership, or that any
  right in §A is held beyond its stated class;
- holding any name, key, or personal data before every `[CONFIRM]` is
  supplied and the pre-opening items of §F are receipted.

## The counter-case

**A rights table is still words.** Yes — and the class column says which
rows are held by something other than words, and the pre-opening
rehearsals are what move a row from `DECLARED` to held. A table without
classes would be the failure `THESIS.md` §7 names.

**The founder is still the weakest layer.** True under every option, and
§D says so in its first row. This decision does not end bootstrap; it makes
its residual powers visible and receipted.

**Option 4 is the only real answer and it is not available.** Also true.
The honest sequence is a custodial arrangement now, labelled, with the
entity as a transfer-gate condition — not an entity invented on paper to
make the labels read better.

## Reasons

1. Custody without rights is hosting with a custodian.
2. The rows that can be rehearsed before opening are the rows that will be
   true; the rest are labelled.
3. The residual powers named here are the ones a reader would otherwise
   discover.
