# D-0004 — Name the addresses, and grow the head of the chain

**Status:** DRAFT — awaiting founder adoption
**Class:** CONSTITUTIONAL
**Authority:** founder bootstrap
**Proposal:** [`P-0001`](../proposals/P-0001.md) — the first decision with a
proposal record in front of it
**Date drafted:** 5 September 2026
**Machine projection:** [`D-0004.yaml`](./D-0004.yaml)

## The decision

### A. Brand and addresses

- The brand is **OURS**. Not OUR, not OUR ONE, not a dotted domain — the
  reasons are P-0001 B2–B4, and the shortest of them is that *our*
  describes while *ours* asserts.
- The institution — constitution, records, proof, ledger — stays at
  **`oursorg.com`**, where *org* is simply true, and where the Authority
  Trace already runs.
- Products live at **`our.one`**: `feed.our.one` first, later Cells at
  their own subdomains, and `our.one` itself as the front door of the
  product family. Lockups: `OURS · our.one` for products; `OURS ·
  oursorg.com · the institution compiler` for the institution.
- **Revisit clause.** `our.one` is adopted as the founder's choice on 5
  September 2026, with the founder's stated intent to acquire an
  exact-word address later if the pilot works. If one is acquired, the
  products move by a decision; the institution's address is unaffected.
  The one-letter gap between OURS and `our.one` is recorded in P-0001 as
  the founder's accepted cost.
- `our.one` enters the asset map in `authority/FOUNDING-AUTHORITY.md` §4
  as a project asset — legal owner the founder, technical controller the
  founder's registrar account, intended holder the member institution or
  a purpose-bound holder under the transfer gates of §8. The row's text is
  written under `M-0004`.
- The cutover preconditions in the handoff §4 apply unchanged before any
  DNS change to `our.one`: the ONE business's replacement domain chosen
  and live, routes inventoried, redirects, email, and search planned,
  explicit founder authorization. Nothing in the feed pilot depends on
  that cutover until `M-0003-RELEASE`.
- Defensive names need no decision; registering one is an operational
  purchase by the founder.

### B. The head of the chain

- [`foundation/LIFECYCLE.md`](../foundation/LIFECYCLE.md) is adopted as
  direction: the fourteen stations, the cost column, the ledger.
- **From this decision on, no decision without a proposal.** `P-0001`
  is the first; `D-0000` to `D-0003` record its absence as a gap, and
  that gap is closed rather than backfilled.
- **Adoption is an event:** a status flip committed by the adopting
  authority. The kernel reports a `DRAFT` decision behind a mandate as
  `CHECKED`, and refuses it as `ENFORCED` once the records below are
  corrected.
- **Cost is a column:** the fields in `LIFECYCLE.md` §4 are added to the
  schemas and receipts under `M-0004`; earlier receipts are back-filled
  from provider records where they exist, marked `ESTIMATED` where they
  do not, and left blank rather than invented.

### D. `M-0004` — the housekeeping mandate

One `BUILD` mandate, drafted after this decision is adopted, for the
agent work: the kernel `DRAFT` check (`CHECKED` first); the cost fields in
`packages/schemas` and the receipt back-fill; the `M-0001-RELEASE`

## What this decision does not authorize

- moving the institution's address from `oursorg.com`;
- any DNS change to `our.one` before the cutover preconditions are met
  and the founder authorizes it;
- any product-line name on any surface that no decision has adopted;
- changing the substance of any record during reconciliation — status
  fields only;
- resolving any `[CONFIRM]` fact by inference.

## The counter-case

**A product is being named on a provisional domain.** The founder has said
`our.one` may be replaced. Accepted: only products sit there, the
institution does not, and the revisit clause makes the move a decision
rather than a scramble. A first-year fee already paid is a reason to use
the domain, not a reason to keep it.

**Reconciliation relabels history.** It does not. The labels are corrected
to what was true, the correction is receipted here, and the wrong period
is stated rather than erased. Erasing would be rewriting; this is the
opposite.

**Adopting the founding authority with facts still `[CONFIRM]`.** Adoption
here means the structure, the control map, and the prohibitions are in
force. Every unverified fact stays marked. That is more honest than a
draft nobody adopts while everyone acts on it.

## Reasons

1. The addresses were the last open question before code, and the founder
   has answered it.
2. An outside reader found the DRAFT labels in an afternoon. The next one
   will too.
3. The proposal record and the cost column are what make the ring
   readable end to end; this is the decision that turns them on.
