## The decision

### A. The contact email

**The controller's contact email is `privacy@ctrlai.com`.** The founder says
the address works. `/privacy` and `/power` show it, from the server's
`DATA_CONTROLLER_EMAIL` setting. This settles D-0013 §A's first `[CONFIRM]`.

### B. The representative in the EU (GDPR Article 27)

**Ctrl AI, Inc. designates the founder, Rado Sukala, as its representative
in the EU.** D-0013 §A offered two options: a representative, or an
establishment in the EU. The founder chose the representative. This settles
D-0013 §A's Article 27 `[CONFIRM]`.

**What the law asks of the role.** The sources are listed at the end.

- **A written designation.**
  - Article 27(1): the controller "shall designate in writing a
    representative in the Union".
  - Ctrl AI signs it, and the founder accepts it personally.
  - No public page names a representative until it is signed (see below).
- **Someone in the EU.**
  - Article 27(3): the representative is established in a Member State where
    the people whose data is processed are.
  - A natural person may hold the role (EDPB Guidelines 3/2018, §4(a)).
- **Named where people read it.**
  - Articles 13(1)(a) and 14(1)(a) require the representative's identity and
    contact details.
  - The EDPB says they belong in the privacy notice (§4(a)).
- **Its duties.**
  - It keeps a record of processing activities (Article 30(1)).
  - It answers supervisory authorities and people for Ctrl AI (Article
    27(4)).
  - Corrective measures and fines imposed on Ctrl AI can be addressed to it.
    It is directly liable only for its own obligations, under Articles 30 and
    58(1)(a) (EDPB §4(d)).

**Interpretations, open to correction.**

- **The representative is reached at the same address,**
  `privacy@ctrlai.com`. The founder gave one address.
- **"Rado Sukala" is the name the founder gave for the role.**
  - This record does not read it as settling FOUNDING-AUTHORITY §3's "Full
    legal name", which stays `[CONFIRM]`. An agent must not resolve it.
  - The written designation uses the founder's full legal name and the
    founder's address in the EU.
- **No country and no address is published.** FOUNDING-AUTHORITY §3's
  "Jurisdiction of residence" stays `[CONFIRM]`. `/privacy` says "in the EU",
  and the signed designation is what makes that true.

**Not examined:** D-0013 §A's other option, whether Ctrl AI is also
established in the EU (Article 3(1)). There is no legal review before release
(D-0013 §C). The representative is designated either way.

### C. What the build shows

- **`/privacy` names the representative** from a new server setting,
  `DATA_CONTROLLER_REPRESENTATIVE`, and only while a controller is named.
- **A server without the setting names none.** A controller inside the EU
  needs none, and anyone who runs the code names their own.
- **Nothing else about the founder or Ctrl AI is added** to a public page.

## Prohibited under this decision

- **Naming a representative on a public page** before the written
  designation is signed.
- **Publishing the founder's address or country** before FOUNDING-AUTHORITY
  §3 records them.
- **An agent resolving** any FOUNDING-AUTHORITY `[CONFIRM]`.

## Reasons

1. **A US company serving people in the EU** must name someone in the EU
   whom they and the authorities can reach, unless it is established there.
2. **The founder chose to hold the role.** It adds no cost, in keeping with
   D-0012's low-cost reasoning.
3. **§B's interpretations are written down,** not assumed, so they can be
   corrected.
