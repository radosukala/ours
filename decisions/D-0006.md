## The decision

### A. Direction

Adopt `foundation/THE-LAYER.md` as direction. OURS is the enduring
institution people belong to; it holds, in trust for the people in it,
identity, standing, data, relationships, law, records, money, and names.
An application is a replaceable implementation under a contract — what it
may do, which information it may reach, whose authority permits it — that
never has users of its own. The compiler is the gate that connects the
constituency's decisions to verified changes and admits an application to
run; the runtime holds the resulting permissions. Its economic premise is
stated as a hypothesis, not an absolute.

On adoption, `THESIS.md` is amended in the adopting commit:

- **§1** gains, beside the institution sentence, the candidate: *OURS is a
  layer that belongs to the people in it. Applications are open source and
  replaceable; the people, their data, and their rules are not.* Marked as
  working material until a naming decision.
- **§11** is retitled *Defensibility, and the definition of the product*,
  with one paragraph stating that the assets it lists are what the layer
  holds.
- **§12** gains, as prior art with links the founder confirms before the
  text is adopted: Solid, the AT Protocol's personal data servers, Matrix,
  Nostr, Open Collective, and GitHub's spec-kit — and the narrow claim that
  none of them makes standing, admission by the users' constitution, and
  holding in trust one object with the data layer.

### C. The record is reshaped, not restarted

The founder asked whether the proposals, decisions, and mandates should be
archived and the work started in a different shape. Decided: **no.** The
reasons are in `P-0005`'s alternatives; the shortest of them is that the
record already is the spec, the audit, and the decisions the new sentence
names, and that a record with a first-class `SUPERSEDED` state does not
need amnesia. What changes, and what is deliberately kept apart:

| Do | What |
|---|---|
| **keep** | the kernel, CLI, verifier, schemas, tests; `apps/proof`; the constitution and founding authority; every proposal, decision, mandate, and receipt; `THESIS.md`, `INSTITUTION-COMPILER.md` (as the gate's design), `LIFECYCLE.md` |
| **supersede** | as in §B — status only, text kept |
| **add** | `spec/` and `communities/` under `M-0006`; `layer/` under the later runtime mandate (§D) |
| **apply in the adopting commit** | the README rewritten to lead with what a person keeps, how an application is admitted, how a community decides, and how to verify; `AGENTS.md` §1 with the new sentence; the status flips of §B. Records only; no code |
| **defer** | the move of the institution's own records under one root. It changes paths the loader, the verifier, source references, and the proof artifacts depend on, and it must preserve verification of every existing bundle and every historical reference. That is a migration with its own scope, decided separately after the gate exists. It does not block proving the layer, and it is **not authorized here** |

**Procedure scales with class**, amending `D-0004` §B's *no decision without
a proposal*: `CONSTITUTIONAL` and `CHARTER` decisions require a proposal
with preserved deliberation; `POLICY` and `PRODUCT_MANDATE` decisions carry
their deliberation inside the decision; `OPERATIONAL` work runs under a
standing mandate and produces receipts only. The volume of this record is a
fact about one person doing the work of a legislature, a court, and a
factory during bootstrap; it is not a property of the ring.

The kernel work the reshape rests on — adoption as an event, the
prerequisite check, the report that tells a draft from an authorisation,
cost fields, the release receipt still owed — is `M-0004`'s, under
`D-0004`. This decision adds nothing to that mandate and depends on it.

### D. What this decision authorizes

| Mandate | Class | What it may do |
|---|---|---|
| `M-0006` | BUILD | build **the gate for community records**: the service specification and its record types, the community root, the procedure, conformance, contract, and pin checks, the fixture's specification for founder approval, and the denial suite; **may not** build a runtime, deploy, hold a name, or hold personal data; **may not begin** before `M-0004`'s adoption check exists and this decision and the mandate are `ADOPTED` |
| `M-0005` | OPERATE | assist the first community's founding under the data-handling terms of `P-0003`/`P-0004`; **may not send its first invitation before `D-0007`** |

The runtime, the fixture tenant on it, and the swap are **not authorized
here**. They are authorized by a later decision, of class
`PRODUCT_MANDATE`, after `M-0006`'s receipt is accepted by the founder and
`D-0007` is adopted — so that the implementation runs against a pinned
specification it cannot change, under an authority the gate has checked is
adopted, on opening conditions that name real rights. `M-0006`'s final
receipt proposes that mandate; it does not file it.

### E. `D-0007` — the opening conditions: rights before custody

Before any real person's data exists on the layer and before any
community's name is held, a decision `D-0007` states: the rights people
receive and how each is exercised, with its class at opening
(`THE-LAYER.md` §10.1); who can remove an operator, by what threshold, and
the rehearsed handover; what happens if a holder refuses, and what is lost
meanwhile; the residual powers of the founder, the operator, and the
holder, each a receipted event when used; the holding arrangement, chosen
among options evaluated against replacement, recovery, member rights, and
residual powers — a single second-human holder being one option and not the
default; and the legal review of the privacy notice. Only the founder can
supply the choices. A draft carrying the questions is filed beside this
decision.

### F. The demonstration

The public artifact is the replay of the first founding from its records,
ending in the swap (replace the application, keep everything), the refusal
(an application reaching outside its contract is refused; the same code
copied elsewhere runs, and has nobody), and a regeneration on a clean
machine. This supersedes *proof leads* and *screens of a finished tool* as
the demonstration direction.

## What this decision does not authorize

- any claim that members exist, that anything is member-owned, that any
  protection is absolute, or that OURS holds anything for anyone today;
- holding a name, a key, or any personal data before `D-0007`;
- the runtime, a tenant on it, or the swap — a later decision;
- starting `M-0006` before `M-0004`'s adoption check exists;
- the move of the institution's records under one root;
- deployment of anything under BUILD authority;
- third-party applications, a marketplace, a payment surface, a protocol
  specification, or a second community;
- a general specification language; if `ours.tool/v0.1` starts to become
  one, `M-0006` stops;
- changing the substance of any superseded record;
- adopting any candidate line in `THE-LAYER.md` §1 as copy.

## The counter-case

Recorded because a decision with no recorded counter-case is an
announcement.

**June, again.** The predecessor's June experiment built a layer —
identity, database, auth, cost — and produced no constituency. This
decision leads to a layer. Accepted knowingly, with two mitigations: the
gate is built first and the layer only after, so that nothing runs that
was not admitted; and `M-0005` runs in parallel, so that the layer has
people in it or the failure is recorded as `FAILED` rather than reframed.

**Three decisions where one was drafted.** The first draft of this decision
authorized the whole build and extended another decision's mandate. The
colleague's second review found that its acceptance rested on a check that
cannot tell a draft from an adopted record, that the implementer could
rewrite the rules it was judged by, and that the protections claimed more
than their mechanisms held. Accepted: the build is split so that each step
gates the next on something real — an adoption check, a pinned
specification, rights before custody. The cost is two more decisions of
minutes each under the procedure this decision adopts.

**Two AI reviewers and a colleague agreed.** Agreement among reviewers is
not evidence; `P-0004` said so and it remains true. The evidence is the
first community passing the replacement test.

**OURS becomes the data controller for everything on the layer.** The
largest responsibility this project can take. Mitigation: §E precedes any
personal data; per-community isolation; minimal data; consent at
invitation; deletion on request.

**The platform position.** Between people and software is where app stores
sit. Mitigation is structural: the layer's own exit — a person's pod and a
community's slice as bundles — the open layer software, the arrangement
`D-0007` adopts bound by the transfer gates, and the portability test
applied to OURS itself.

**Restart was considered and declined.** The founder's doubt about the
process is real and is preserved as dissent. If the reshape does not make
the live thing visible first and the ceremony proportionate, restart
remains available, and the record will say why.

## Reasons

1. The samples read as SaaS because they were organised around producing
   and administering applications; what people retain while applications
   change is the product, and the record now says so.
2. The corrections narrow the claim to what can be held, and the classes
   say how.
3. The gate is the only built organ; the record is its authority; both are
   kept because they are the audit the new sentence names.
4. Rights before custody is the difference between a layer and
   founder-controlled hosting with an extra custodian.
