# D-0015 — The front page leads with the product

**Status:** ADOPTED by the founder under bootstrap authority on 29 September
2026. The founder's words, after seeing the prototype P-0009 proposed:

> yes to your prototype, this is much better homepage! Please build it

**How this record was made.** An agent drafted it from those words and from
P-0009. Where it interprets them, it says so. Before the build, a cold-read
critic found six sentences that said more than the facts, and one question
the page should answer; §J lists what the prototype said and what the page
says instead. The founder's review of
the exact wording is **PENDING**, and an amendment after that review is a
new record.

**Class:** CONSTITUTIONAL. It amends `D-0012` §D, the message, which is of
that class.
**Authority:** founder bootstrap
**Prerequisites:** `D-0012`
**Proposal:** [`P-0009`](../proposals/P-0009.md)
**Machine projection:** [`D-0015.yaml`](./D-0015.yaml)

## The decision

### A. The headline

**"Just your people. Then you're done."** It replaces D-0012 §D's *"Today
it's mine. At [threshold] members, I give it away."* The metadata title is
*"our.one · Just your people. Then you're done."*

*Interpretation, open to correction:* the prototype offered three headlines
and opened on this one, the agent's recommendation. The founder approved
the prototype and did not choose between them. The second, *"Remember when
it was just your friends?"*, stays available, and the five-stranger test
can decide between them before release.

### B. The lede

*"our.one shows you posts from the people you choose, newest first. No ads
and no suggested posts. When you've seen them all, it tells you, and you
can put your phone down."*

It replaces "No ads. No ranking." on the front page. "No ranking" stays in
promise 3 on `/contract`, and on `/rules`.

### C. The promise, signed, on the first screen

The first screen carries the founder's promise under the Get in form, as a
card signed "Rado, maintainer":

*"I'll never sell our.one. When [threshold] people have joined, I hand it to
a not-for-profit body of its members, and they can replace me."* (corrected
before the build; see §J)

It restates promises 1 and 2 of the contract in the founder's voice. The
claims scan lists it by exact text, as M-0011 requires of every sentence
about the handover. The scan's rule widens to catch "hand it to", which it
could not see before.

### D. The count, and the seats

**The count keeps D-0012 §D's form:** a rank, *"N people are in. You'd be
#N+1."*, with no progress bar. It moves from the first screen to the
section on who runs our.one. There it measures the distance to the
handover, which is what it means.

**The seat line shows only when no seat is open.** It reads *"No seats open
right now. Leave your address and you'll get the next one."* A count of
open seats is no longer shown. *"Have an invite? Open the link you were
sent."* stays only while joining is closed, when an invite is the only way
in.

### E. Where your friends went, with its source

A section headed *"Where did your friends go?"*:

- **The words:** *"In January 2025, content from friends got 7% of the time
  Americans spent on Instagram. Most of the rest went to short videos from
  strangers, recommended by AI."*
- **Its source:** the court's opinion in *FTC v. Meta Platforms*, No.
  1:20-cv-03590 (D.D.C.), Memorandum Opinion, ECF No. 705 (the public
  version, filed 2 December 2025; first issued 18 November 2025 as ECF No.
  693), page 8. The page links it.
- **The passage, as the agent read it on 29 September 2026:** "Americans
  now spend only 17% of their time on Facebook viewing content from their
  friends. See DX 1152 (Jan. 2025 Facebook Surface Breakdown). On
  Instagram, that number is 7%. See DX 1153 (Jan. 2025 Instagram Surface
  Breakdown). What has replaced content from friends? For the most part,
  short videos posted by strangers and recommended by AI."
  - Source: https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.224921/gov.uscourts.dcd.224921.705.0.pdf
- **`[CONFIRM]`:** a human confirms that the source says what the sentence
  cites (AGENTS.md §10). Until then the sentence may be built and tested,
  but not published.

Beside it is an illustration: a ranked feed that keeps going, next to an
our.one feed that ends. It is labelled as an illustration.

### F. How it works, and the story

- **How it works,** in three steps: get in, bring your people, catch up and
  close it. It says that the feed is quiet until your people are there.
- **The story:** WhatsApp in three dated lines (2012, 2014, 2025), each
  linked to its source in SPEC §18.9. Then D-0012's line, word for word:
  *"An owner can sell it, change it or shut it down. A maintainer does the
  job, or is replaced."* The founder's own line heads the section: *"Keep
  your people. Change who runs it."*

### G. What stays from D-0012 §D

- **The idea:** maintainers, not owners.
- **The status line**, in every footer.
- **The three things every page that makes the promise also says:**
  - what holds each promise today;
  - that before the threshold it is a promise;
  - what happens if the threshold is never reached.
- **The handover promise** in its listed words:
  - *"When [threshold] people have joined, I hand over our.one's domain,
    its data and the right to replace whoever runs it to a not-for-profit
    body of its members, founded by their vote."*
  - *"Then nothing is handed over."*

### H. What is not changed

**The answer after Get in stays as it is:** *"Check your email. If a seat
was open, your link is there. If not, you're in line, and we'll write when
one opens. If this address already has an account, just sign in."*

- **The prototype showed a shorter answer:** *"Check your email. We've sent
  you the next step."*
- **The agent's note P-0009 relied on said the shorter answer is true in
  every case. That was wrong.** An address that already has an account,
  and an address that waits in line, receive no email when they ask
  (`src/core/seats.ts`).
- **Making it true** means sending an email in both cases. That changes
  what `/privacy` says is sent, so it is left for a separate decision.

**Also left for later decisions:**

- the invite page;
- a first step after joining that invites three people;
- photos.

### J. Corrections made before the build

A cold-read critic read the prototype against the contract, the claims
scan and AGENTS.md before the build. These sentences said more than the
facts, and the page says the second version:

| Where | The prototype said | The page says | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The signed card | "…I hand it to its members, and they can replace me." | "…I hand it to a not-for-profit body of its members, and they can replace me." | The contract hands the domain, the data and the right to replace the maintainer to a body the members found. "To its members" reads as ownership, which D-0012 dropped. |
| Where your friends went | "…spent 7% of their time on Instagram looking at posts from friends. The rest was everything else." | §E's words | Closer to the court's words, and the second sentence is the court's own finding. |
| The same section | "On our.one there is nothing else." | "On our.one, your feed is only the people you chose, and then it ends." | It is the feed that has nothing else. |
| Under Get in | "We use your email only to send you the link." | "We'll email you the link. Once you've joined, you also get a weekly email, which you can stop. What we keep, and for how long, is in Privacy." | The weekly email is on by default for a new account (`src/core/schema.ts`). The old front page said "only" as well. |
| The phone | "That's everything from your people since yesterday." | "You've seen everything from before your last visit, 2 days ago." | It is what the app's marker says (`src/components/Marker.tsx`). |
| How it works, step 1 | "Your email and a name. It's free." | "Your email, a name and a username. It's free, and you need to be 18 or older." | Joining asks for all three and for the 18+ confirmation. |
| Fair questions | (none about photos) | "Can I post photos?" "Not yet. Posts are words for now." | `/rules` says "for now, no photos", and the Instagram comparison would lead a visitor to expect them. |

**Kept, against the critic:** *"An owner can sell it, change it or shut it
down. A maintainer does the job, or is replaced."* The critic's objection
is that today nobody can replace the maintainer. The line is D-0012's,
adopted word for word, and the same section says what holds each promise
today. It stays open for the founder's review.

## Prohibited under this decision

- Publishing the sentence of §E before a human confirms its source.
- Any sentence about the handover that is not listed by exact text.
- Stating the handover as done.
- An answer after Get in that is untrue for any address.

## Reasons

- **Five launches found no users.** The front page is where a stranger
  decides, and P-0009 found that it answers trust before want.
- **The substance stays.** The maintainer, the contract, the handover
  promise and the status line are unchanged. What changes is their order
  and their place.

## Dissent

None recorded. The agent recommended testing the page on five strangers
before building. The founder chose to build first. The test is still open,
and it should run before release.

## Appeal

None available during bootstrap. Recorded as a known gap.
