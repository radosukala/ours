**Status:** ADOPTED by the founder under bootstrap authority on 1 October
2026. The founder's words, after seeing the prototype P-0010 proposed:

> yes build it, and fix the footer too

**How this record was made.** An agent drafted it from those words, from
P-0010 and from the prototype (`docs-internal/prototype/front-v4.html`,
not published). Where it interprets them, it says so. The prototype was
built on the page as it stands, from the review the founder shared on 1

anything it does not name stays as `D-0015` left it. After the build, two
verifiers checked it; §N lists what they found and what changed.

## The decision

### A. The lede

*"A social network for your friends and the people you choose to follow.
Their posts, newest first. No ads and no suggested posts. When you've seen
them all, it tells you, and you can get on with your day."*

It replaces `D-0015` §B's. The page now says what our.one is before it says
how it works. Following is part of the app (`src/core/schema.ts`,
`src/core/feed.ts`), so the sentence is true of it.

### B. Joining

- **The button** says *"Join our.one"* when a seat is open, or when the
  seats can't be read. When no seat is open, it says *"Join the waiting
  list"*. It replaced "Get in": the form only ever joins, and an address
  that already has an account gets no email from it (`D-0015` §H).
- **The heading of that part of the page,** read by screen readers only, is
  *"Join our.one"*.
- **The seat line,** only when no seat is open: *"No seats are open right
  now. Seats go to whoever has waited longest."* The button now says what
  the line used to ("Leave your address to join the line").
- **How it works, step 1,** is titled *"Join"*. Its text is unchanged.
- **The close,** only while the form is shown:
  - the heading *"Who would you like to hear from?"*;
  - the line *"Join, then send them an invite."*;
  - a link that looks like a button, with the form's label, to the form.

  It replaces "Bring your people." and "Get in".
- **Unchanged:** the answer after a valid submission (`D-0015` §H); the
  free line; the privacy note; the two lines while joining is closed.

### C. The signed card

*"I'll never sell our.one. When [threshold] people have joined, I hand over
its domain, its data and the right to replace me to a not-for-profit body of
its members. Until then, I hold all three."*

Signed as before: *"[maintainer], maintainer · How that works"*.

It replaces `D-0015` §C's card, which handed over "it", all of our.one. The
contract hands over three things (promise 2, `/contract`), and says the
maintainer holds them until then. The verifiers of `M-0013` raised this for
the founder (`D-0015` §K). The third sentence keeps the promise and its
present limit together.

### D. The order on a phone

One column, in this order: the headline, the lede, joining, the picture of
the feed, then the card. A visitor sees the product before the promise.
From 900px wide nothing moves: the words and the card on the left, the
phone on the right.

### E. The reason

The section *"Keep your people. Change who runs it."* opens with:

> **The people make the network.**
>
> You bring the friendships, the conversations and the reasons to come
> back, so you should have a say in what it becomes. A simple feed is where
> our.one starts. The bigger purpose is a network whose people choose who
> looks after it.

*Interpretation, open to correction:* the review wrote these thoughts as
"we", for the people and the founder together. The agent wrote them in the
founder's voice, which the rest of the page uses. It left out the review's
heading, "They should have the final say". The handover gives a narrower
thing, the right to replace whoever runs it, and the last sentence names
that.

### F. WhatsApp in 2025

The third dated line is *"WhatsApp announced ads in Status, in its Updates
tab."* It links to Meta's announcement of 16 June 2025:
https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/helping-you-find-more-channels-businesses-on-whatsapp/

It replaces *"Ads came to WhatsApp."*, linked to a news report. The
announcement keeps those ads in the Updates tab, away from personal chats,
and rolls them out over months. The old line said more than that.

### G. What holds the promise today

- **The paragraph about the maintainer** ends: *"…The rest can change only
  with [notice] days' notice. In Settings, you can download your profile,
  posts, replies and connections, and delete it all."* It replaces "…and
  you can always leave with everything." The new words are the contract's
  promise 4, and what the export holds (`src/core/export.ts`). "Everything"
  could be read as moving the whole network elsewhere.
- **The present state:** *"Today I hold the domain, the data and the keys.
  The members' body has not been formed, and the handover has not
  happened."* It replaces "Today these promises are held by that contract,
  not yet by law." The facts are the contract's own: until [threshold], the
  maintainer holds the domain, the data and the keys.

### H. Two answers

The other five questions and their answers are unchanged.

### I. The invite page

`D-0015` §H left the invite page for a later decision. This is that
decision, for its words only:

- **The pitch** a signed-out visitor reads, both while joining is closed
  and while it is open, is the front page's lede of §A, word for word. It
  replaces "A home for friends and people you choose to follow. Their
  posts, in order, with an end when you're caught up."
- **While the visitor can join,** under the form: *"Free to join."* and a
  link, *"The promise behind our.one"*, to the section on who runs it
  (`/#front-runs`).
- **Unchanged:**
  - the heading that names the inviter;
  - adding a friend;
  - the form, and its answers;
  - "Already on our.one? Sign in, then open this link again."

  Accepting an invite stays a friend request and nothing more.

### J. The status line

*"Maintained by its founder. Promised: when [threshold] people have joined,
its domain, its data and the right to replace the maintainer go to a
not-for-profit body of its members."*

It replaces `D-0012` §D's *"Maintained by its founder. Handed to its members
at [threshold]."*, which `D-0015` §G kept. It stands wherever that line
stood: every footer, on the public pages and beside the app; the lede of
`/power`; and `/costs` and `/rules`.

*Interpretation, open to correction:* the founder's words name the task, not
the sentence. The agent had asked whether to bring the footer in line with
the card. This line does that:

- it names the same three things and the same recipient;
- it says, in its first word after the present state, that this is a
  promise;
- it no longer reads as done. "Handed to its members" did, and the claims
  scan let it through on every page.

  nobody else holds it today.
- **Accepting an invite.** §I and `M-0014` say it "stays a friend request".
  It makes the two friends at once, as it did before this build, and

**Polish, from the rendering verifier:**

**Accepted, not fixed,** and recorded in the verifiers' tests:

- **An address that already holds a seat.** When its link expires, its

- **The status line's "not-for-profit"** can still break at its hyphen at

**For the founder.** These are open, and not decided by agents:

  - The agent proposes "when [threshold] people are in", the words of the

  - It would change `D-0012`'s handover sentence, so it waits for the

- **Five readings the verifiers left to a person:**

  - whether "you should have a say in what it becomes" says more than the

  - "Join, then send them an invite." beside "Join the waiting list";

## Prohibited under this decision

- Publishing `D-0015` §E's 7% sentence before a human confirms its source.
- Any sentence about the handover that is not listed by exact text, here or
  in the claims scan's list.
- Stating the handover as done.
- An answer after joining that is untrue for any address.

## Reasons

- **A visitor should learn what our.one is, why it exists and whom to bring
  in one page.** The page built under `M-0013` did the first well, the
  second only as a promise, and the third impersonally.
- **Four lines said more than their facts or less than they could.** Each
  now says what its source says.
- **The card and the status line now say the same thing,** and both say
  exactly what the contract hands over.
- **The substance is unchanged:**
  - the maintainer, the contract, the handover promise;
  - the count;
  - the answer after joining;
  - the 7% sentence's `[CONFIRM]`.
