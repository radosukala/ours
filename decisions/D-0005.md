**Machine projection:** [`D-0005.yaml`](./D-0005.yaml)

## The decision

Amend the v0 option catalog in `foundation/FEED-PILOT.md` §6 and the
rulebook table in `envelopes/M-0003/A-records-and-kernel.md` §1 so that OURS
Feed shows a person the minutes they have spent in the feed — this session
and today — by default, kept on their device only, under a new article
`R-TIME-SHOWN-NOT-KEPT`.

The option is built under the existing mandate `M-0003`. This decision
authorizes no new mandate; it changes what that mandate's Part A must
record and what its Part C must build.

## Why

Engagement-funded feeds measure how long a person stays and hide the number
because the number is the product. OURS Feed is not paid by attention. The
honest consequence is to show the number and refuse to keep it — and to do
so by default, because a default is the product for most people.

This option's catalog record carries `decision: D-0003`, `mandate: M-0003`,
`proposal: P-0002`, `amended_by: D-0005`.

## What this decision does not authorize

- storing, sending, or syncing any reading-duration value beyond the
  person's device;
- using time spent as an input to ranking, notifications, or any other
  behavior;
- making the option `FLOOR` — the person may turn it off;
- any change to the catalog beyond the one row and the one article named.

## The counter-case

**It adds a metric to a product that refuses metrics.** Accepted, and
answered by the article: this is the person's number, on the person's
device, shown to the person. The product cannot see it. The refusal was
never of measurement; it was of measurement that serves someone else.

**A default changes before a single person has used the product.** True,
and it is the reason to decide now rather than later: an option added
before Part A runs enters the catalog with a proposal and a decision behind
it, which is what every option is supposed to carry. Added after, it would
carry a patch.

## Reasons

1. The number the incumbent hides is the number a person needs most.
2. Showing it costs nothing to privacy when it never leaves the device.
3. Deciding before Part A gives the option a proposal's provenance.
