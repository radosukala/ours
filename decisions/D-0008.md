## The decision

Authorize one `BUILD` mandate, `M-0007`, for the v0 runtime of the layer,
the fictional community's booking tool running on it as a tenant, and the
rehearsed replacement of that tool — the swap — with the community's
records intact. Fictional data only. Nothing deployed. No real person's
data, no community's name, until `D-0007`'s opening conditions are
rehearsed and a release mandate says so.

## Why

`M-0006` built the gate and proved it on a fixture's records: a charter
reads as a root, a vote is counted against its charter, a pinned
specification cannot be changed by its implementer, and an implementation
reaching past its contract is refused — statically. What does not exist is
the thing the gate admits *to*: a runtime that holds a community's data,
hands an application a capability scoped to its contract, checks every
disclosure at the boundary, and lets the application be replaced while the
records stay. `THE-LAYER.md` §7 states the protections; §8 states the
replacement test; neither is held by anything until this runs.

`D-0006` §D put this decision after two things: `M-0006`'s receipt accepted
by the founder, and `D-0007` adopted. The first happened on 8 September
2026, in conversation, with the founder's approval of `spec/SPEC.md` and
the fixture; the independent verification of `M-0006` is filed beside its
conformance report. The second is the founder's to supply, and this
decision waits for it.

## What this decision does not authorize

- any real person's data on the layer — the tenant's data is the fixture's,
  invented and labelled;
- deployment to any environment; a release is a separate mandate after
  `D-0007`'s pre-opening rehearsals;
- a second community, a third-party application, a marketplace, or a
  payment surface;
- changing the fixture's specification, charter, or pin — an amendment is
  the fixture's own decision, re-pinned;
- any claim that confinement holds beyond what `M-0007`'s tests show, or
  that anything is owned by anyone.

## The counter-case

**A runtime holding fictional data proves confinement, not ownership.**
True. It proves the mechanism `THE-LAYER.md` §7 claims, on data nobody
owns; ownership is `D-0007`'s question, and this decision cannot precede
it, by its own prerequisite.

**The swap on a fixture proves the mechanism, not a community's ability to
use it.** True. The replacement test is passed by the first real community
or not at all; the fixture shows that the machinery can carry records
across, which is necessary and not sufficient.

**June, again — a layer with nobody in it.** `M-0005` runs in parallel and
brings the first community, or records that it did not. This decision
builds what that community would be admitted to, not a platform waiting
for tenants.

## Reasons

1. The gate without a runtime admits applications to nothing.
2. The protections of `THE-LAYER.md` §7 are declared until a boundary
   exists to hold them.
3. The swap is the architectural test of the direction, and it can be
   rehearsed on invented data before any real person is asked to trust it.
