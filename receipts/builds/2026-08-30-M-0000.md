## Beyond the mandate

Two things were done that M-0000 did not name, both recorded here rather than
absorbed silently:

1. **`ajv` and `ajv-formats` were removed** after being scaffolded. The kernel
   validates with typed checks, not JSON Schema, so they were unused. The
   mandate's dependency policy is justify-new; an unjustified dependency is
   removed, not kept in case.
2. **TypeScript was pinned to 5.9.3** although 7.0.2 is current. TS 7 is the
   new native port; the first day of this repository is not where to discover
   its edge cases. This is a reversible choice and should be revisited.

## Failures during the build

  could not resolve `@ours/*`. Fixed by declaring them as root
