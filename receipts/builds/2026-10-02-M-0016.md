## What was built

- **`kit/`, what a coding agent needs to build a project for our.one:**
  - `build.md`, the instructions it follows, step by step;
  - `our-one.mjs`, one file with no dependencies:
    - `init` sets a project up;
    - `check` runs rules version 0's ten checks, each with its class, and
      lists what a person must read and the safeguards not built;
    - `rules` prints the rules block;
    - `--json` and `--hook` (Claude Code's stop hook);
  - `our.one.schema.json`, a README, and the Apache-2.0 licence (the
    feed's).
- **The feed under the same check:**
  - `apps/web/our.one.json` declares what `/privacy` says the feed keeps,
    word for word, plus seat requests;
  - its outside services are Resend, and Vercel and Neon, which D-0013
    chose for hosting and the database, in words true before the deploy
    and after it (since the verification);
  - `src/core` and `scripts` are its boundary;
  - `apps/web/AGENTS.md` carries the rules block.
- **`/build`, "Build on our.one":**
  - the two ways to build, side by side;
  - the one line for a coding agent;
  - what the agent does;
  - the rules and how each is checked;
  - what the check can and can't tell;
  - the proposal, by email through `/maintainers`;
  - the tools, with the tool's version and SHA-256.
- **Routes:** `/build.md`, `/kit/our-one.mjs` and `/kit/our.one.schema.json`
  serve the kit's files from the build, byte for byte.
- **Links:**
  - the footer's "Build with us" goes to `/build`;
  - `/maintainers` and `/projects` link to it;
  - `/projects` says the feed passes the check.
- **The claims scan** reads the kit's text.
