## What was built

- **`/`, the front door:** *"The software we live in should be ours."*,
  the two entrances, what holds today, the picture; the idea; the feed as
  the first project, with its join form, the count and the two labelled
  possibilities; what "ours" means, as proposed, with the illustration;
  the builders' invitation and the line for a coding agent; what's in the
  open; and the ways to take part.
- **`/feed`:** the front page's words, form, seats, picture, promise and
  questions, moved unchanged. Signed-in visitors go to `/home` from both.
- **The public layout and identity:** the header's four places and Sign
  in; the footer's line; paper, ink, an acid field and a rust accent, light
  and dark, in the device's own fonts. The signed-in app, and the two
  pictures of it, keep their look.
- **The drafts:** a need or an idea, in the browser; copied, or opened in
  the visitor's own email app while `PROPOSALS_EMAIL` is set. Nothing is
  saved, sent or counted. Without JavaScript, the buttons lead to
  `/maintainers`.
- **`/projects`, `/build`, `/maintainers`, `/privacy`:** as D-0020 §B and
  §E say.
- **`kit/build.md`:** the idea first, in `PITCH.md`, then the person's
  choice to find out first or to build now. The new line for a coding
  agent, in `kit-info.ts` and `kit/README.md`.

**Unchanged:**

- the feed's verified words, its contract and the common agreement's words;
- the rules, the tool and the schema;
- any data, schema or provider.

**Two things D-0020 didn't name, done so its words stay true:**

- **The front door shows the count and the feed's join form,** because
  `/contract` counts "the number on the front page" and the seat email
  says to ask again "on the front page". Changing either would have
  changed the contract or a mail. The lines under the form moved to
  `join.ts`, which both pages read.
- **Older tests adapted,** each with its reason in a comment: those that
  rendered `/` as the feed's page now render `/feed`; those that pinned
  one navigation now expect the header's too; the colour tests allow the
  pictures' restated app tokens; and M-0016's `/build` tests follow
  D-0020 §D.
