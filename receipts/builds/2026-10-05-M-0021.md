## What was built

- **The first screen (§A),** in this order: the headline; *"It starts with
  a friends feed: your people, newest first, and then it ends. No ads."*;
  the public count against its threshold, *"1 of 100,000"*, with the
  pledge's own sentence; the status line; one form. *I want to build* is a
  text link to `/build`; *I want this to exist* is gone, as the form is the
  entrance. The founder saw these words before the build (D-0024 §E).
- **The count (§B):** `memberCount`, as the contract counts; a number,
  never a bar; the sentence alone when it can't be read; the same number
  in the panel beside a member's feed, *"N of 100,000 people are in."*
- **The form (§C):** the address, an optional *"Which app would you take
  back?"* of at most 140 characters, D-0016 §B's button, the lines under
  it, and *"N apps named so far."* from the first one.
- **A need kept:** in `takeSeat`, read first, refused at its field when too
  long, kept only after the seat request went through, without the
  address, in a table of its own (migration `0003_needs`, with its down
  file). Read by the founder in the database; no code lists the words.
- **The privacy notice's row** *"Apps you name"*, and the kit manifest's.
- **The link card (§D):** `openGraph` and `twitter` metadata on every page,
  and `public/card.png`, rendered from `scripts/card.html` by `pnpm card`
  and committed.

**Unchanged:** `/feed`'s verified words, the contract, the agreement, the
drafts, the seats' and the waiting list's rules, and what the feed does;
the records, the kit, any provider, domain or key.

**Older tests adapted,** nineteen of them, each with *"Changed under
M-0021"* and its reason: those that pinned the old lede and entrances, the
front door's links into its sections, the claims scan's files and listed
sentences, the schema's and the privacy notice's tables, the migrations'
count, and the member's links on the front door.

  screen; D-0024 §A's list doesn't name it.
