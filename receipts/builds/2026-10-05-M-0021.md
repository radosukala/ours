## Found while building, and fixed

- **In a browser, three things the tests could not see:** the form's one-line
  layout (made for one field and a button) crushed three children into two
  columns, so the address and the button were unreadable; React empties a form
  after every submit, so a refusal at the need wiped the address too; the
  pledge's quotation kept the browser's 40px side margin. All three are fixed,
  with tests.
- **In the suite, a production-only defect:** the privacy right's sentence,
  written as JSX text after bold words over two lines, would have lost its
  space in `next build` (*"can't be found.If you answered"*). The repo's
  existing test for exactly this caught it; it is now a string expression.
- **A test of mine** asserted the wrong thing about a log line; fixed.
- **A pre-existing race, recorded, not fixed here:** `verify-abuse`'s *"thirty
  reports fired at once stop at exactly twenty"* failed once, in one full
  run, with 21 and 9. It passes alone (3 of 3), passed in the baseline run on
  `ac38742`, and passed in the two full runs after it (`30f1cc8`, `c9ac8bc`). The cause: `hit()` (`src/core/limits.ts`) counts rows
  with `created_at <= now`, and each concurrent caller passes its own `now`,
  so a caller stamped earlier that takes the lock later does not count a
  later row. It is outside M-0021's scope; a separate task is queued, and the
  first thing it asks for is a mandate.

## The deploy

**At this commit, nothing is pushed.** A push to `main` is a deploy on this
project (the M-0020 receipt: `main` is still Vercel's production branch), and
M-0021 and D-0024 name *"the founder's go, after screenshots"* for it
(AGENTS.md §8: BUILD does not become DEPLOY).

The founder's instruction of 5 October 2026 was *"finish M-0021 and commit to
main"*. It is read here as asking for the verified build to be landed on
`main`, not as a review of the open choices above, and it came before the
screens existed. So: the screens went to the founder first (five of fifteen);
the push waits until the verification has closed with every finding fixed or
recorded, and one green run of the whole suite on the final tree; and what was
done is written here, below, when it is done.

**What a push does and does not do.** Vercel builds `main` and serves it. The
migration `0003_needs` is **not** applied by a push: `release.ts` applies it
only in a production build of the one commit the founder names in
`OURS_RELEASE`. Until then the site runs this code on the old schema, and the
form asks for the address alone, with no question and no count of named apps
(tested: the table dropped under the route). Nothing is lost by the order, and
nothing asks a visitor for what can't be kept. To turn the question on, the
founder names the commit in `OURS_RELEASE` and rebuilds, as for `38250a0`.

## Rollback

Revert the M-0021 commits, and run `apps/web/drizzle/down/0003_needs.sql` by
hand, in one transaction, on any database that applied `0003_needs`. Read the
words first if they are wanted: the down migration drops them. What a revert
can't undo: the words named while it was live, and the card X, Facebook and
Slack kept of the page.
