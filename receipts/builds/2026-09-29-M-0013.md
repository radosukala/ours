## What went wrong on the way

- **The prototype's shorter answer after Get in was untrue.** The agent's
  note said *"Check your email. We've sent you the next step."* was true in
  every case. It is not: an address that has an account, or waits in line,
  receives nothing (`src/core/seats.ts`). It was not built (D-0015 §H).
- **The cold-read critic found six overstatements in the prototype.**
  - the card handed our.one "to its members";
  - the privacy note said "only", though the weekly email is on by
    default;
  - the phone used words the app never shows;
  - step 1 left out the username and the 18+ confirmation;
  - two sentences went beyond the court's words.

  All were corrected before the build (D-0015 §J).
- **Three suites failed on the first full run.** Each had pinned the old
  copy:
  - the claims samples keyed by the rule's source;
  - the join-terms sentence;
  - the widened rule's catch in `/auth`.

  Each was retargeted, and the change is in the diff.
- **One expectation in the new tests was wrong** (the illustration's two
  labels are not adjacent in the text). It was corrected.
- **The first phone screenshots were blank.** The app forbids framing
  (`frame-ancestors 'none'`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`), so the page could not
  be framed at phone width. They were retaken in the built-in browser.
- **Headless Chrome did not exit after screenshotting an http page.** Each
  run was stopped once its file was written.
- **The verifiers found one HIGH and ten smaller defects** in the page as
  first built, among them a seat line that promised the next seat when
  seats go to whoever has waited longest, and a phone that showed a state
  the app never draws. All are fixed or recorded; see the verification
  report and D-0015 §K.
- **The re-checker found the fixed phone showing like counts** on other
  people's posts, which the product refuses (SPEC §7), and an invite line
  that promised what an expired invite would break. Both fixed.
- **The verifiers' test files were adapted before being committed as
  written.** The re-checker reconstructed the originals from the agents'
  transcripts to diff them. Recorded in SPEC §18.15 as a process gap.
