## The first line, as the mandate requires

Before this mandate's own check existed, `pnpm ours check M-0004` at commit
`0671bb8` printed `AUTHORISED`. That word meant *well-formed* and nothing
more: the kernel of that commit could not tell a draft from an adopted
record. Execution authority for this work came from the founder's adoption
of `D-0004` (commit `d717eb7`) and of `M-0004` (commit `0671bb8`), stated
in conversation on 8 September 2026 and applied as status flips by the
agent. After this build, the same command prints `AUTHORISED FOR
EXECUTION`, and a draft prints `VALID AS A DRAFT — not authorised for
execution` with the draft named.

## What was built

1. **The adoption check** — `R-TRUTHFUL-STATUS/adoption`, `ENFORCED`. A
   mandate is refused while its own status, its decision's, or the founding
   authority's is `DRAFT` or `PROPOSED`; the message names every draft in
   the chain. The `CHECKED` phase the mandate provided for was not needed:
   the reconciliation of `D-0004` §C was committed at `d717eb7` before this
   mandate ran, so the switch point is that commit and the check has been
   `ENFORCED` from its first run.
2. **The prerequisite check** — `R-SOURCE-HIERARCHY/prerequisite`,
   `ENFORCED`. A decision (`prerequisite_decisions`) or a mandate
   (`authority.prerequisite_decisions`) may name decisions that must be
   adopted first; a missing or draft prerequisite is a refusal, and the
   message names it. `D-0006`, `D-0007`, and `M-0006` already carried the
   field as `DECLARED`; it is now read.
3. **The report tells the two apart.** `summarise()` gains `execution` —
   `AUTHORISED_FOR_EXECUTION`, `VALID_AS_DRAFT`, or `REFUSED` — and
   `waitingOn`, the adoption and prerequisite messages behind a draft. The
   CLI ends in one of three lines and never a bare `AUTHORISED`; it exits
   `0` only for execution authority, so a process gating on the exit code
   cannot proceed on a draft. The verifier re-derives adoption from the
   bundle's own records and fails a draft bundle, saying it is valid as a
   draft.
4. **Denial coverage** — `tests/adversarial/adoption.test.ts`, thirteen
   tests: an `ADOPTED` mandate under a `DRAFT` decision; a `DRAFT` mandate
   under an `ADOPTED` decision; a `DRAFT` founding authority; every draft
   in a chain named, not only the first; a missing prerequisite; a draft
   prerequisite; an adopted prerequisite passing and saying which; the
   three summary states; every mandate in `mandates/` yielding one of the
   three and no draft yielding execution authority; the verifier failing a
   draft bundle and noting adoption on the real one.
5. **Cost as a column** — `CostFields` on decisions and mandates,
   `limits.budget_usd`, and `prerequisite_decisions`, all `STRUCTURAL` and
   not evaluated. The three receipts of 30 August gain a cost section:
   token and money columns blank, because no provider record was retained;
   wall-clock upper bounds for `M-0001` and `M-0002` from commit
   timestamps, marked `ESTIMATED`; nothing invented.
6. **The layout and the asset map.** The README already lists
   `proposals/` and `envelopes/` — rewritten in `D-0006`'s adopting commit,
   `0671bb8` — so no change was needed here. The `our.one` row in
   `FOUNDING-AUTHORITY.md` §4 now carries `D-0004` §A's words, with the
   previous entry stated rather than erased.
7. **The receipt still owed** —
   [`receipts/releases/2026-08-30-M-0001-RELEASE.md`](../releases/2026-08-30-M-0001-RELEASE.md).
   The served page was fetched and hashed; a clean worktree at the
   build-configuration commit reproduced it byte for byte once the build
   date the page embeds was normalised. That is reproduction modulo one
   declared field, not digest equality, and the receipt says so with the
   class it deserves: `CHECKED`, by a person, once.
