  - `/` and `/feed` no longer send a member to `/home`;

## For the founder to decide

- **Whether a member's links stay in view as a page scrolls** (R12): from
  700px the header scrolls away with the page, and the bottom bar shows
  only on a phone. A sticky header, or the bar at more widths, is a design
  choice.
- **The session's id in the server's log** when the database is down,
  found outside the verification's lenses and older than M-0020: the
  subject of a record of its own.
- **A member's header on the public pages.** M-0020 says the public header
  shows a member *"Your feed"*, and the build does that. D-0023 §B says
  that where a visitor has *Sign in*, a member has *"their feed, their
  notifications and their own pages"*. Read that way, the public pages
  would carry the app's links too, and on a phone the bottom bar. The
  reading that asks for more work is not taken here (AGENTS.md §12); the
  founder chooses.
