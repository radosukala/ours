## What was built

- **One identity (§A):** the whole site's tokens are our.one's, light and
  dark: paper `#f5f3eb`, ink `#222b24`, rust `#bf411d`, the acid of the
  panel's buttons, the wordmark with its rust dot, and the public pages'
  type. The icon, the manifest and the browser's bar follow. None of X's
  colours (`#1d9bf0`, `#f91880`, `#0f1419`, `#536471`, `#eff3f4`) remains
  in the stylesheets or the pictures of the app.
- **One header (§B):**
  - `SiteHeader` on every page: the wordmark, which goes to `/`, and the
    four places;
  - in the app, `MemberLinks` on its right: Feed, Notifications and People
    with their counts, and a menu with Your profile, Settings and, for
    administrators, Moderation;
  - on a phone the bottom bar stays, with the feed named the feed;
  - from 761 to 999px the app's header puts the four places on a row of
    their own, as a phone does;
  - no left navigation, no Post pill.
- **The front door for members (§C):**
  - `/` and `/feed` no longer send a member to `/home`;
  - the public header offers a member *"Your feed"*;
  - where a visitor is asked to join, a member is shown *"You're in."* and
    *"Open your feed"*, and the count without a rank.
- **The feed as the first project (§D):**
  - it is called *Feed*;
  - beside it, or after it below 1000px, the panel: the line, *"This feed
    is our.one's first project. What should we make ours?"*, Name a need
    and Bring an idea (the front door's drafts), and the four places.
