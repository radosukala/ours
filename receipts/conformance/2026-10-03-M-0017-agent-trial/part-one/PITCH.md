# Proposal: linden-tools

## The need

The 40 or so households on Linden Row sometimes need a tool they don't own, such as a drill, a ladder or a pressure washer. Today people ask in a WhatsApp group, where requests get lost, or they buy tools they use twice a year.

## What it offers

A tool-lending library for Linden Row. Neighbours list the tools they're happy to lend, and others borrow them for a few days. You join with your name, your email and your house number (just the number), and you sign in with a link sent to your email. Neighbours who've joined see each other's names, house numbers and tools. Only the lender and the borrower see a loan: who borrowed what, and when. Loans are kept for a year, and when you leave, your account's data is deleted. No payments, no AI, no analytics.
