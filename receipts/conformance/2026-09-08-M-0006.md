Corrections to the report above, each traced to the verification's item:

- **§1, `R-NO-FICTIONAL-OWNERSHIP`.** The command the section is headed
  with printed *open*, because the CLI passed no public text; the "held"
  was the suite's. `check --root` now scans every human source under the
  community root, and the command as headed prints *held* over the
  fixture's nine files.

  non-negative integer; dissent must be preserved; standing is compared
  only when its record predates the vote's close.
- **§3.** Unchanged: every `T-` article is still `DECLARED`, named and not
  run.
- **§4, the gate's rules.** Two rules added — `S-CHARTER-ADOPTED`,
  `S-CHARTER-GRANTS` — and `S-CONTRACT-DECLARED` rebuilt to refuse by
  default; its row read "held over `booking-ok`: 1 file, 5 client calls"

  `pins: true` and to be of this community. `S-TOOL-NAMED` refuses a draft

  each.
- **§6, the suite.** 95 was right then; it is 124 now, across 8 files.
