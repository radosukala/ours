# Verification — M-0017, the front door

**Build:** `e4e5637` (the front door, `/feed`, the drafts, the reworked
pages and `build.md`'s idea step), under [`M-0017`](../../mandates/M-0017.md),
from [`D-0020`](../../decisions/D-0020.md).

**Stopping rule, declared before verification starts:**

1. Two independent verifiers, neither of whom built the front door:
   - one for **honesty**: every sentence the front door, `/feed`,
     `/projects`, `/build`, `/maintainers`, `/privacy`'s new sentence and
     `build.md`'s idea step put in front of a person or an agent, against
     the records (D-0015 to D-0020) and against what the code does. Above
     all: nothing that says user control, the holder or a safeguard
     exists; every possibility and the illustration labelled; no status
     that turns false at the deploy; no pay, audience or income promised;
     the feed's verified words unchanged on `/feed`;
   - one for **rendering and use**: every public page in a browser at
     320, 375, 768, 1280 and 1440px, light and dark; by keyboard alone (the
     header, the tabs, the illustration, the drafts' dialog and where focus
     goes); without JavaScript; and the drafts' denial paths (nothing sent,
     stored or counted; email only with `PROPOSALS_EMAIL` set; nothing
     loaded from another origin).
2. **An agent trial:** a fresh agent, given only the new line on `/build`
   and a FICTIONAL person's answers, against a local production build. The
   person first wants to find out whether people want the idea, then
   decides to build. Does the agent draft the idea before any code, stop
   when asked, and reach READY TO PROPOSE when told to build?
3. Their tests committed as written, before any fix.
4. Fixes.
5. One re-check, by an agent that built none of it.
6. Then every finding is fixed or recorded here. There is no further round.

**What this verification is not:** the message test. Agents reading the
pages are not strangers, and nothing here says how people will understand
the message. The revised test (D-0020 §C) runs with people, before the
public launch.

**Classes:** every check here is `CHECKED` (a test reports). Only `R-SCOPE`
is `ENFORCED`.
