## The stopping rule, declared before it starts

This is the rule M-0018 used, as M-0020 asks.

1. **Two verifiers that built none of it,** each in its own worktree
   outside the main checkout (its `apps/web/.env.local` may hold real keys,
   D-0021 §F):
   - **honesty:** every sentence and label M-0020 added or changed, and
     every page a member now sees: the panel beside the feed, the member's
     header and menu, the bottom bar, the naming of the feed, the front
     door and `/feed` for a member, SPEC §18.22 and the build receipt.
     They are checked against D-0023, M-0020's acceptance criteria one by
     one, AGENTS.md and what the code does. The feed's verified words stay
     unchanged;
   - **rendering and use:** every signed-in page in a browser at 375, 820,
     1000 and 1440px, light and dark, and at 320px for overflow. By
     keyboard alone: the header, the member's menu, the panel's drafts and
     the bottom bar. The front door and `/feed` as a member and as a
     visitor; the public pages with the database down; the contrast of the
     new colours.
2. **Their tests are committed as written,** before any fix: each finding
   is a `DEFECT` test that fails on the verified commit; each sound check a
   `closed` test that passes.
3. **Every finding is fixed or recorded.** A fixed `DEFECT` test is renamed
   *"fixed ("*; a test adapted to a fix says *"Changed after…"* and why.
4. **One re-check** by an agent that built and verified none of it.
5. **Every re-check finding is fixed or recorded,** and there is no further
   round.

**What the founder asks for is not a finding.** The screenshots went to
the founder before this started, with one question named in the build
receipt (a member's header on the public pages). An answer that changes
the build, or a change the founder asks for, is made under a record before
the re-check, written here, and re-checked with the rest.

**Classes:** every check here is `CHECKED` (a test reports). Only `R-SCOPE`
is `ENFORCED`.
