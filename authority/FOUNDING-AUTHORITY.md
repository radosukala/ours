## 1. Why this document exists

A compiler cannot emerge from authority it has not yet encoded. OURS proposes
that legitimate human authority should be the source of software, and OURS
does not yet have members. Something has to be the first source.

That source is the founder, acting alone, and this document says so plainly
rather than staging a ratification by a constituency that does not exist.

**Bootstrap truth is stronger than fictional democracy.**

## 2. The declaration

The founder is the sole source of authority in this project today.

No member has authorized anything. No vote has occurred. No membership has
been issued. No legal instrument binds any asset to any member. Every
decision recorded in this repository before member formation carries founder
authority and nothing else, and every public surface must say so.

### A correction, kept rather than overwritten

An earlier version of this section recorded the registered office as **800
North King Street, Wilmington, DE 19801**, from the founder's statement on 30
August 2026. The Certificate of Incorporation gives a different address, and
the certificate governs. The earlier entry was wrong.

It is recorded here rather than quietly replaced, because it is the clearest
evidence available for why the evidence column exists. The fact was marked
*stated, unverified* for roughly twenty minutes before the governing document
contradicted it. Had it been written down as fact, this declaration would now
assert something false about the entity it names.

### What is still not established

**Quoting a certificate is not reading the filing.** These values come from
text the founder supplied. Confirming them means retrieving the entity record
from the Delaware Division of Corporations and citing its file number, which
no one has done. Until then the evidence column says what it says.

**A registered office is a service address, not a place of business.** The
address is that of Legalinc Corporate Services, Inc., a commercial registered
agent. It establishes the state of incorporation and where process is
accepted. It says nothing about where the company operates, who directs it, or
what it holds.

**No relationship to OURS is established.** The entity is named in the
foundation package as a *possible* transitional operator. Nothing here records
that it operates, owns, funds, or employs anything connected to this project.
Until a verified record says otherwise, no document in this repository may
state that it does — and the fact that its details are now written down does
not change that.

## 5. What the founder may decide during bootstrap

The founder may:

- adopt, amend, and supersede the Constitution and this declaration;
- issue decisions and mandates in every class;
- appoint and remove stewards and operators;
- authorize builds, releases, and reversals;
- choose infrastructure, providers, and legal counsel;
- spend the founder's own funds;
- stop the project.

## 6. What the founder may not claim

While this declaration is operative, no surface of this project — code,
copy, receipt, README, social post, or presentation — may state or imply
that:

- OURS is member-owned, or that members exist;
- anyone other than the founder has ratified anything;
- a legal membership interest has been issued;
- member authority reaches any asset;
- the founder's control has been transferred, escrowed, or constrained by
  anyone else;
- any gate in this repository is non-bypassable by the founder.

The last one deserves its own sentence. **The founder can currently remove
every check in this repository without notice.** Every enforcement claim made
here is bounded by that fact, and saying so is a requirement rather than a
disclaimer.

## 8. Sunset

This declaration is not permanent, and its expiry is not a date. It ends when
its conditions are met.

Founder bootstrap authority ends, in whole or in defined parts, when all of:

1. a legal member institution exists and can hold assets;
2. membership has actually been issued to people who are not the founder or
   the founder's household;
3. the constitution and member rights are legally operative, not merely
   published;
4. asset control — repository, domain, brand, infrastructure, treasury — has
   moved to the institution or to a purpose-bound holder;
5. a decision procedure exists that the founder cannot unilaterally override;
6. an appeal path exists that the founder does not decide.

Until every one of those is true, the correct public status is the one in
§2 of `AGENTS.md`, unchanged.

## 9. Review

This declaration is reviewed whenever any `[CONFIRM]` is resolved, whenever a
sunset condition is met, and at minimum whenever the Constitution is amended.
