## 2. The summary, as pasted by the founder

> My view: the project has become more substantial than its presentation.
> The homepage still introduces a quiet friends feed. The builder pages
> explain participation mostly through rules. The larger opportunity—people
> bringing useful services into existence and retaining control—is
> difficult to discover.
> I built a new interactive presentation (`docs-internal/prototype/our-one-next.html`), and left it open for you.
>
> The direction I would test is “The software we live in should be ours.”
> It gives the feed a place inside the ambition, while making builders part
> of the invitation immediately.
> The proposal includes:
>
> * Distinct entrances for people who want something better and people who
>   can build it.
> * The feed as the first project, with clearly labelled possibilities
>   beyond it.
> * An interactive explanation of changing maintainers while retaining
>   relationships and shared records.
> * The real builder kit, a copyable starter prompt, and a clear
>   explanation of the potential paid role.
> * Public evidence of what exists, what remains a draft, and what still
>   needs building.
>
> The most important improvement is to the participation model. Your
> current agent instructions still lead through build → check → propose.
> That risks repeating the problem you identified: builders investing in
> software before discovering whether anyone wants it.
> I would introduce “Draft an idea first.” Let builders describe an offer
> and find interested people before completing the product. Let users
> describe what would make an alternative worth trying. The kit then helps
> prepare promising work for review. The prototype demonstrates both
> entrances.
> That creates a more concrete invitation to share: “This is something we
> want. Would you try it with us?” It gives people a reason to bring others
> beyond supporting an abstract mission.
> I would also revise the message test. Its current definition of
> “understood” favours recognising a social feed. We should test whether
> people understand the wider proposition, the maintainer opportunity, and
> what is available today.
> The preview works locally on desktop and phone. I also reran the kit
> against the feed: its checks pass, while runtime protection and
> collective control remain unfinished.
> The full review and proposed implementation scope (`docs-internal/our-one-next-review-2026-10-02.md`) are saved. Production remains
> unchanged: D-0018 (line 86) (`decisions/D-0018.md`, line 86) says the
> headline and lede “stay as they are until the five-stranger test is
> run.” This is a concrete direction to evaluate against that decision.
