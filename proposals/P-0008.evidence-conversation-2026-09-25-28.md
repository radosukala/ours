# P-0008 evidence — the founder's words, 25–28 September 2026

The founder's messages below are verbatim, in the order he sent them.
The agent's answers are summarised after each one, with the drafts they
point to. Those drafts sit in `docs-internal/`, which is not published: it
is the founder's scratch space. What they contain that this proposal relies
on is restated in `P-0008.md`.

>
> I think that as feed is our first product and feed could work even for
> small group of friends, we doesnt need to be usefull to let everybody
> migrate. What could also work for influencers, as we will deliver to
> their fans feed 100% of their message, with one middle size influencer
> supporting our.one it could take off.
>
> I'm still also not sure about branding to have name Ours and then domain
> our.one ... whether we shouldnt unify branding, to be crystal clear what

**The agent's answer** (`docs-internal/the-contract.md`):

- **The contract:** yes, as long as what matters leaves the maintainer's
  hands. Ello's 2014 charter promised no ads and no data sales and bound
  future owners, yet the company was sold quietly in 2018 and the charter
  deleted. A Delaware public benefit charter can be enforced only by
  shareholders holding at least 2% (8 Del. C. §367).
- **The name:** *maintainer*, yes.
- **Creators:** they come after the promises hold.
- **Branding:** one name, our.one.

**The agent's answer** (`docs-internal/the-message.md` and
`the-message.essay.md`):

- **The idea:** "Maintainers, not owners". No existing campaign uses the
  phrase. "Public benefit software" is avoided because Ello was a public
  benefit corporation.
- **The story:** WhatsApp's arc from 2012 to 2025.
- **What bold means here:** an irreversible act, plus a contrast people
  recognise, plus an idea bigger than the product.
- **Distribution:** invitations, the Czech press and Hacker News.

## 3. 27 September: not a small body, and a threshold

> I like it ... just few points. I'm not sure with find 2 more and transfer
> domain ownership mechanics ... aren't we able to unlock it with some
> mechanism when more people will join? for instnace 100k people? to not be
> able attacked by just a few people? Or whether we can have ownership more
> independent on maintainer? I suppose that we plan that codebase will be
> opensource, only assets maintainer protect for now is domain and
> database. How to manage this better? what option do we have?

**The agent's answer** (`docs-internal/the-holder.md`):

- **Two kinds of protection:**
  - *custody* protects against the maintainer;
  - *control* protects against capture by a few, and grows with
    membership.
- **Custody:** an independent holder owns every account, and the maintainer
  only has access.
- **Control:** a member ladder at 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000.
- **The structure:** a Czech nadační fond as the holder, plus a members'
  spolek.

## 4. 27 September: global and cheap

> wait, I'm not sure to have czech foundation, if our ambition is a global
> project. Don't we really have some clean, global, cost effective option?
> Until we will proof concept that people will resonate with this idea and
> would like to join our.one I want to keep cost low, not to spend too much
> on lawyer or structure. On the other hand we won't attract people
> probably on promise only, in the future domain and database will be owned
> by people :(

**The agent's answer** (`docs-internal/the-light-path.md`):

- **Custody:** The Commons Conservancy, a free Dutch non-profit that holds
  open projects' rights, would hold the domain under a one-page statute.
- **The database:** it stays with the founder until a trigger, and the
  status line says so.
- **The fallback:** a UK community interest company.

> I agree with proposals with one caveat, for the last years we built

> that project has a chance. You know, the world is different place for the
> last 12 months as anybody can code today and build project.
>
> I don't want to break rules, I just would like to execute all the
> transfers of rights what we promise after project will proof that our
> idea resonate with people. I would like to start now, strong message,
> working app and ownership transfer executed for instance if we will
> achieve 100k or 1M members to show our bold ambition.
>
> What we need now is the MESSAGE that will resonate, build website,
> launched.

**The agent's answer** (`docs-internal/the-launch.md`):

- **The front page:** "At 100,000 members, it's ours", with a live counter.
- **Custody and control:** the contract is the Terms. Before the threshold,
  the founder holds everything and says so. At 100,000 there is a handover
  to a members' not-for-profit, founded by their vote.
- **The threshold:** 100,000 rather than 1,000,000, because a million
  reads as "never".
- **The build:** the website, under a new BUILD mandate.
