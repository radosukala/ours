# P-0011 — evidence: the founder's words, 1–2 October 2026

Verbatim, as the founder wrote them in the conversation with the architect
(an agent). Spelling and punctuation are unchanged. The architect's answers
are summarized in `P-0011.yaml` under `deliberation`. Its working drafts
(`docs-internal/the-maintainers.md`, `docs-internal/the-framework.md`) are
founder scratch and are not published.

## 1. 1 October: the question

> I'm still not 100% sure with how we are building ours ... start with feed,
> user owned, maintainer ... all sounds ok, but we are probably let money on
> the table, with opportunity to bring also builders to our ecosystem. Take
> how many people is trying to build app, to make a living, with zero
> chance. I think that many people could be aspiring maintainers ... if they
> will build and manage something with prospect of some financial payment
> ... we can still promise that once maintainer could be paid job, but maybe
> human creativity and hard work, and todays ai coding capability, if enough
> users will get together could rebuild many, many apps and services.
>
> Lets try to think harder, deeper, more revolutionary than only friends
> feed.

## 2. 1 October: "yes to direction"

The founder's answer to the architect's first answer (a network whose
members pay maintainers, with the feed as its first app):

> yes to direction ... now to more thoughts and opinions. With rejected old
> version because it was "10 person community collecting $10" ... I think
> that is not right optics. Yes there could be apps and services for small
> communities where maintainer won't be paid, but if progress of AI
> development will continue with present pace, massive apps could be build
> with AI if app will replace paid app with milion users and there could be
> cases for how to pay one maintainer of whole teams. we could touch masive
> markets if we solve well user ownership.
>
> The most important part I think is that user ownership will be simply and
> well defined and understandable. But we also need to well define well
> possible future for maintainers (present builders in hard times to let
> the world know about their products). They are the one that could help us
> a lot to build and bring their audience or to let the world know about
> our idea and movement. I won't push and build it alone. I think that we
> cannot start with feed only. We should have clear framework how people
> could aspire to be maintainers and pitch what they want to solve for
> users.
>
> I simple think that message shouldn't be this is feed, if there will be
> 100k of you I will give away it to you ... and maybe if it will work there
> will some other project and maintainers. Feed should be just the first
> project, with the same framework of ownership as other, with me as
> maintainer, not paid for now .
