## The problem

Three things arrived on one day and belong to one decision.

**1. The direction.** `P-0004` moved the entry point to *a community's
shared tool*. The founder's reflections of 8 September go past the entry
point to the definition: the samples still read as SaaS because they were
organised around producing and administering applications, and the thing
worth being as large as the ambition is what people **retain while
applications change** — identity, standing, data, relationships, rules,
records, money, names. When code is cheap, the users are the only asset an
app has; OURS is the layer where the constituency owns that asset itself,
and an app is a replaceable tenant with a contract. The founder's own
architectural requirement: a person's profile and data must not live inside
an app that can be taken away with it.

**2. The corrections.** A colleague reviewed the agent's draft and kept its
centre — *the ownership layer is the centre, the compiler is its gate* —
while correcting three things the draft got wrong: a single central holder
was called "stronger" when the requirement is *replaceability with rights
preserved*; "the data cannot be taken" was too strong, since scoped access
does not prevent misuse within a grant; and the swap needs a durable
**service specification**, not merely "satisfies the same charter". The
review adds that technical admission does not grant a constituency, and
gives a synthesis sentence.

**3. The process.** The founder asks whether the present record —
proposals, decisions, mandates — is worth keeping, or whether the job
should now be started and shaped differently, as *a GitHub spec plus
ownership and rules, the constitution applied, then test and audit of any
app someone submits*.

## Assessment

### Where the founder and the reviewer are right

- **The layer is the product; the compiler is its gate.** `THESIS.md` §11
  already makes the argument; `INSTITUTION-COMPILER.md` §18–§19 already
  separate the tiers of data and define the exit pack; the June 2026
  `our.one` repository already said *the shared layer is the actual
  product; the apps are outputs*. What changes is the centre of gravity:
  these were properties of an institution and become its definition.
- **The reviewer's three corrections are adopted in full**
  (`THE-LAYER.md` §5, §7, §10) and improve the claim by narrowing it.
- **No factory.** Spec-driven pipelines carry a spec to code; OURS
  supplies what the spec derives from and where the code runs. The agent's
  earlier proposal of an OURS build stage is withdrawn.
- **The swap is the architectural test** (`THE-LAYER.md` §8).

### Where the record must sharpen what was said

- **"Central database" is the v0, not the definition.** The founder's
  requirement — profile and data outside the app — is right and is §4's
  reason for existing. The reviewer's requirement — replaceability with
  rights preserved — is the definition, and the formats must allow
  plurality of holders and operators even though v0 has one of each.
- **"No chance for others to run it as SaaS" is not a claim this project
  may make** and not one it should want. Forking is a right, including from
  OURS. The honest claim is that the people, the records, and the rules
  cannot be taken without consent, and that the running instance is
  preferred for as long as it deserves to be (`THESIS.md` §11).
- **"GitHub spec plus ownership plus audit" is the shape — and the current
  record already is most of it.** The spec is `packages/schemas` plus a
  human specification; the audit is the kernel plus conformance; ownership
  is the layer. The proposals, decisions, and mandates are not overhead
  beside the product: they are the *decisions* the reviewer's sentence
  says the compiler connects to verified changes, and the mechanism the
  workshop screens showed a community using. What is heavy is applying full
  ceremony to the founder's own three-line changes, and what is dead is the
  feed. Both are fixed by reshaping, not restarting — see the alternatives.

### What the review misses

- **The holder is the first decision, not the last.** A layer without a
  holder is the June experiment with better prose. `D-0006` therefore
  gates personal data and names on a separate holder decision, `D-0007`,
  which only the founder can fill in.
- **The reviewer's spec-kit remark is stronger than it says.** Its
  constitution is the builder's engineering law and its spec the builder's;
  its placeholder governance articles and acceptance criteria are exactly
  where a community's articles project. Read on 8 September 2026 by the
  agent; links unconfirmed (`R-CHECKABLE-CLAIM`).

**`D-0006`:**

1. **Adopt `foundation/THE-LAYER.md` as direction**, with the amendments to
   `THESIS.md` §1, §11, §12 it lists, and its economic premise stated as a
   hypothesis.
2. **Supersede** `D-0003`, `M-0003`, `D-0005`, `FEED-PILOT.md`, and the
   `M-0003` envelopes, retained in full; `SHARED-TOOL.md` becomes
   `SUPERSEDED` as direction with its community-facing sections carried
   forward; `FIRST-PRODUCT.md` §7.6 superseded.
3. **Reshape, do not restart:** keep the kernel, the verifier, the
   constitution, the founding authority, and every record; add `spec/` and
   `communities/` under `M-0006`; apply the records-only changes — README,
   `AGENTS.md` §1, status flips — in the adopting commit; **defer** the
   move of the institution's records to a separate migration decision with
   a scope that preserves verification of existing bundles; scale
   procedure to decision class. The kernel work is `M-0004`'s, under
   `D-0004`, and `D-0006` adds nothing to it.
4. **Authorize `M-0006`, BUILD — the gate for community records, bounded:**
   record types, checks, the pin, a fictional community's specification
   for approval, the denial suite. No runtime; nothing runs. It may not
   begin before `M-0004`'s adoption check exists.
5. **Authorize `M-0005`, OPERATE** — the first community's founding, with
   `P-0003`/`P-0004`'s data-handling terms unchanged and its first
   invitation gated on `D-0007`.
6. **The runtime, the tenant on it, and the swap** are authorized by a
   later decision, after `M-0006`'s receipt is accepted and `D-0007` is
   adopted — so that the implementation runs against a pinned specification
   it cannot change, under an authority the gate has checked is adopted.
7. **The demonstration** is the replay of the first founding, ending in the
   swap, the refusal, and a regeneration on a clean machine.

**`D-0007` — the opening conditions, rights before custody:** the rights
people receive at opening with who holds each, how it is exercised, and its
class; operator removal and its rehearsal; what happens if a holder
refuses and what is lost meanwhile; residual powers named and receipted;
the holding arrangement chosen among evaluated options, none presented as
ownership; legal review before any personal data. The answers are the
founder's; the questions are filed.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Counter-case |
|---|---|
| **restart the repository and the process** in a new shape | it would discard the only built organ (the gate, with its denial suite) and the honest history (the scope violation, the wrong address, the DRAFT-while-acted-on period) that is this project's credibility; a third clean start in three months is itself evidence a reviewer reads; and the from-scratch layout is about seventy percent of the existing one — supersession is a first-class state in this record and is what a reshape uses |
| keep the compiler at the centre (round five) | a better app factory is still an app factory; it leaves the users where SaaS leaves them |
| build an OURS build stage | commodity, replaceable, and a distraction from the distinctive work; the reviewer and the founder agree |
| one central holder as the definition | judged against the replaceability requirement it is weaker, not stronger; v0 has one holder and the formats allow many |
| publish the layer as a protocol first | a specification nobody has used; extracted from the first community, as `P-0004` already said of the protocol |
| a manual founding first, then the layer | proves demand and nothing about the layer; the first tool's data would be typed into the wrong place — the founder's own criterion for the wrong architecture |
