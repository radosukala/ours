There is a broader product issue too: **the opening condition still
establishes a holder more clearly than it establishes user ownership.**
D-0007 should explicitly identify the rights people actually receive, how
they exercise them, who can remove an operator, and what happens if a
holder refuses. Otherwise, the fixture can demonstrate replacement
successfully while the real service remains founder-controlled hosting
with an additional custodian.

The foundation also retains the unsupported absolutes "every app owns its
users" and "constituencies [are] the only scarce thing." These should
become a narrower economic hypothesis before adoption. They weaken an
argument that does not need them.

**I agree with preserving the history and superseding obsolete directions.
I would separate that decision from moving the repository and authorizing
the entire build.** First correct the trust boundaries and ownership
opening conditions; then adopt the direction and issue a bounded
implementation mandate.
