## A. Meta, Threads, Cambridge Analytica, reach, parents

1. **FTC v. Meta.** In January 2025, US users spent **17% of their Facebook time and 7% of their Instagram time on friends' content.** The figures come from Meta's own trial exhibits, "Jan. 2025 Facebook/Instagram Surface Breakdown" (DX 1152/1153), which Judge Boasberg cited. Since January 2023 the friends' share fell "by almost a quarter" on Facebook and "by more than a third" on Instagram. Meta argued that it competes with TikTok and YouTube and that people "mostly use Facebook and Instagram to watch unconnected videos". The ruling of 18 Nov 2025 found that "Meta holds no monopoly in the relevant market." The FTC appealed on 20 Jan 2026; the status of the appeal was not checked.
   - https://assets.bwbx.io/documents/users/iqjWHBFdfxIU/rww8JGP.20cc/v0
   - https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/01/ftc-appeals-ruling-meta-monopolization-case
2. **Recommended content, from Zuckerberg's earnings calls.**
   - 27 Jul 2022: about 15% of Facebook feed content was recommended from accounts you don't follow.
   - 26 Apr 2023: more than 20%, and about 40% across Instagram.
   - 24 Apr 2024: about 30% of posts on the Facebook feed, and more than 50% of what people see on Instagram.
   - Meta's Widely Viewed Content Report for Q2 2026 (US Facebook Feed): 36.6% of views were recommended posts from unfollowed accounts, and 20.2% came from friends.
   - Sources: Meta Q2-2022, Q1-2023 and Q1-2024 earnings call transcripts (s21.q4cdn.com); https://transparency.meta.com/reports/widely-viewed-content-report/
3. **Threads.** You log in with your Instagram account and can follow the same accounts (Meta, 5 Jul 2023). "100 million people signed up for Threads in five days" (Mosseri, 10 Jul 2023). Similarweb's estimate of daily users (Android, worldwide) fell from 49.3M on 7 Jul to 10.3M on 7 Aug, down 79%.
   - https://about.fb.com/news/2023/07/introducing-threads-new-app-text-sharing/
   - https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/10/instagrams-threads-app-reaches-100-million-users-in-just-five-days/
   - https://www.similarweb.com/blog/insights/social-media-news/threads-first-month/
4. **Cambridge Analytica.** Data on "up to 87 million people" may have been improperly shared (Facebook, 4 Apr 2018). About 270,000 people downloaded the app, which gave it their data plus "more limited information about friends" (Facebook, 16 Mar 2018).
   - https://about.fb.com/news/2018/04/restricting-data-access/
   - https://about.fb.com/news/2018/03/suspending-cambridge-analytica/
5. **Organic reach.** Socialinsider, 3 Sep 2026, measured brand pages only (872,075 posts). Mid-2026 reach was 1.80% of followers on Facebook and 4.90% on Instagram. Smaller accounts reach a larger share: 6.65% on Instagram for 1–5K followers. This is vendor data, and the article's summary gives different figures. **No verified study of creators was found.**
   - https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/
6. **Parents and law.**
   - Pew, 22 Apr 2025: 55% of US parents are extremely or very concerned about teen mental health, and 44% of those name social media as the biggest negative influence.
   - Pew, 11 Mar 2024: 76% of parents say managing their teen's phone time is a priority.
   - Australia's under-16 rule has applied since 10 Dec 2025.
   - European Parliament, 26 Nov 2025: a non-binding call for a digital minimum age of 16.
   - European Commission, 17 Sep 2026: a proposed "EU KIDS Act" with no social media under 13 and no own account until 15. It is a proposal, not law.
   - Czech consent age for online services is 15 (Act 110/2019 §7).
   - Czech PM and deputy PM backed an under-15 ban on 9 Feb 2026; no bill was found.
   - Sources: pewresearch.org (both); esafety.gov.au; europarl.europa.eu; digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu; zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2019-110; english.radio.cz

## B. Networks that made this promise before, and member-run infrastructure

1. **Diaspora** (2010): raised about $200k on Kickstarter from about 6,500 backers against a $10k goal, and had 180k users by Nov 2011. The founders handed it to the community in Aug 2012.
   - https://techcrunch.com/2010/06/02/diaspora-project/
   - https://techcrunch.com/2012/08/27/diaspora-founders-hand-project-to-community/
2. **Ello** (2014): invite requests reached 31,000 an hour, and up to 45,000 at peak. It adopted a public-benefit charter: "Ello must never make money from selling ads." Talenthouse bought it in 2018. **In June 2023 its servers failed and nine years of posts were lost without warning.**
   - https://www.upi.com/Science_News/Technology/2014/09/30/Social-network-Ello-getting-thousands-of-requests-per-hour/7801412055087
   - https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ello-becomes-a-public-benefit-corporation-and-legally-vows-to-forever-remain-ad-free-founders-and-investors-sign-charter-376792333.html
   - https://waxy.org/2024/01/the-quiet-death-of-ellos-big-dreams/
3. **Vero** (no ads, chronological): grew from fewer than 200k users to nearly 3M within a week in Feb 2018. Outages and a backlash followed. It still operates.
   - https://www.voanews.com/a/vero-instagram-alternative/4278121.html
   - https://time.com/5178976/vero-app-ceo/
4. **Path** (friends-only): claimed 10–15M users. Kakao bought it in 2015, and it closed on 18 Oct 2018.
   - https://www.engadget.com/2018-09-17-path-private-social-network-stickers-dead.html
5. **BeReal:** daily users fell from about 15M (Oct 2022) to about 6M (spring 2023). Voodoo bought it for €500M, announced 11 Jun 2024. From Jul 2024, EU users who refused ad tracking were asked again every day, and noyb complained to the French regulator on 12 Dec 2024.
   - https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/11/bereal-is-being-acquired-by-mobile-apps-and-games-company-voodoo-for-e500m
   - https://noyb.eu/en/bereal-app-wont-take-no-answer
6. **MeWe:** gained 2.5M members in one week in Jan 2021, then downloads fell more than 80% by Feb 2021.
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MeWe
7. **2025–26 launches in the same space:**
   - **UpScrolled** (chronological): #1 US social-networking app on 28 Jan 2026. Daily users fell from 138.5k to 68k within six days, and it struggled to moderate hate speech.
     - https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/11/upscrolleds-social-network-is-struggling-to-moderate-hate-speech-after-fast-growth/
     - https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/03/tiktok-recovers-from-dip-in-usage-that-benefited-rival-apps-following-u-s-ownership-change/
   - **Friendster relaunch** (Apr 2026): friends are added in person, with no ads and no algorithm. Its user numbers are UNVERIFIED.
   - **Yope:** $12.3M seed round; private, no algorithms, no ads; about 15M registered users; subscription (TechCrunch, 22 Jul 2026).
     - https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/22/yope-raises-12-3m-to-build-a-private-social-network-without-algorithms-or-ads/
8. **social.coop:** founded in 2017. Members pay sliding-scale dues of £1–£10 a month, the budget is about £12.9k a year, and it had 434 monthly active users on 24 Sep 2026.
   - https://opencollective.com/socialcoop
   - https://social.coop/api/v2/instance
9. **Blacksky:** community-run AT Protocol infrastructure. It says it has 2M users. It is funded by donations, about $6k a month against a $7.5k goal.
   - https://opencollective.com/blacksky
   - https://newpublic.substack.com/p/how-blacksky-grew-to-millions-of
10. **Europe:**
    - **Eurosky:** EU-hosted AT Protocol identity and data servers, launched 16 Apr 2026 and run by a Dutch non-profit, Stichting Modal.
      - https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/04/16/eurosky-europe-aims-to-rival-big-tech-with-its-own-social-media-ecosystem
    - **Mastodon:** moving to a European non-profit.
      - https://blog.joinmastodon.org/2025/01/the-people-should-own-the-town-square/
    - **W** (Sweden): identity-verified users only, beta in Jun 2026.
    - **Gander** (Canada): built on AT Protocol; $2M raised from 2,517 crowd-investors.

## C. AT Protocol, Signal, EU portability

1. **Bluesky / AT Protocol.**
   - **Users.** About 46M registered (TechCrunch, 11 Aug 2026). Similarweb estimates 10.4M monthly users in June 2026, down 27.2% on the year, and about 3M mobile daily users in July 2026.
   - **Feeds.** The Following feed is chronological; Discover is the algorithmic default.
   - **Private data.** **All repository data is public, and so are follows and blocks.** "Permissioned data" was the 2026 roadmap focus. The "Atproto Spaces" alpha shipped on 20 Aug 2026 for developers only, with launch targeted later in 2026. **No private or friends-only posts, and no private follows, were found in production.**
   - **Portability.** Accounts can move between providers while keeping their identity (DID), with caveats.
   - **Apps on the same graph.** Skylight has 380k+ users, and Bluesky says over 1,000 AT Protocol apps are used each week.
   - **Ownership.** Bluesky is a PBC owned by Jay Graber and the team. It raised an $8M seed (Neo), a $15M Series A (Blockchain Capital) and a $100M Series B (Bain Capital Crypto, Apr 2025).
   - Sources:
     - https://atproto.com/specs/repository
     - https://atproto.com/blog/atproto-spaces-alpha
     - https://atproto.com/blog/2026-spring-roadmap
     - https://bsky.social/about/blog/10-24-2024-series-a
     - https://bsky.social/about/blog/03-19-2026-series-b
     - https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/blueskys-active-user-base-is-shrinking-as-its-focus-expands-beyond-the-app/
2. **Signal.**
   - **Costs in 2023.** Infrastructure cost about $14M a year: registration $6M, servers $2.9M, bandwidth $2.8M, storage $1.3M. Staff cost about $19M for about 50 people. It projected about $50M a year by 2025.
   - **FY2024** (Form 990): expenses $38.0M, contributions $21.8M, liabilities $35.7M.
   - **Users.** Signal publishes no count. About 70M active users is a third-party figure (Lawfare, Feb 2024).
   - **DERIVED cost per user per year:** infrastructure about $0.14–0.20, and everything about $0.38–0.71, for 70–100M users. This is rough because the user count is unofficial.
   - Sources:
     - https://signal.org/blog/signal-is-expensive/
     - https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/824506840
3. **DMA Art 6(9).**
   - **The obligation.** Gatekeepers must give users, and third parties the user authorises, effective portability free of charge, "including by the provision of continuous and real-time access".
   - **Meta's tool.** Meta's "Export Your Information" does recurring transfers of photos, videos, posts and events to an authorised destination: daily, weekly (since June 2026), monthly or yearly, for 1–3 years. It has **no dedicated social-graph type**. One receiver says it gets Instagram followers and following lists. Friend entries historically held names and timestamps only.
   - **Other people's data.** The draft Commission–EDPB joint guidelines (Oct 2025, final version expected Q4 2026) say Art 6(9) also covers other people's data in the dataset.
   - Sources:
     - https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2025-10/joint_com-edpb_gls_interplay_dma_gdpr_for_public_consultation_en.pdf
     - https://developers.facebook.com/docs/data-portability/onboarding-guide/
     - https://developers.facebook.com/docs/data-portability/changelog/
4. **GDPR portability guidelines** (WP242 rev.01, endorsed by the EDPB in 2018). Records naming other people, such as contacts, may be exported to the user. But a receiving service may process them "only to the extent that the data are kept under the sole control of the requesting user and is only managed for purely personal or household needs". It must not use them to "rebuild his social environment, without his knowledge and consent".
   - https://ec.europa.eu/newsroom/dae/redirection/document/44099
