# P-0015 — Where our.one runs: North America

**Status:** ADOPTED, by the founder's words of 3 October 2026, as recorded
in [`D-0022`](../decisions/D-0022.md).
**Requests:** D-0022 (OPERATIONAL)
**Proposed by:** the founder
**Drafted by:** an agent, from the founder's words
**Evidence:** [`P-0015.evidence-conversation-2026-10-03.md`](./P-0015.evidence-conversation-2026-10-03.md)

## The problem

- **M-0012 asked for a database in an EU region,** and D-0021 §D put the
  functions in Frankfurt beside it.
- **The founder's database was made in Ohio** (Neon, us-east-2), and the
  founder expects most people to come from outside Europe.
- **The repository pins the functions to Frankfurt** (`apps/web/vercel.json`),
  over the region set in Vercel's dashboard. So the deployed site ran in
  Frankfurt with its database in Ohio, and every page crossed the
  Atlantic.

## What is proposed

1. **our.one runs in North America:** the functions in Cleveland (`cle1`),
   beside the database at Neon in Ohio (us-east-2).
2. **The feed's manifest says where its data is kept,** and the site names
   Cleveland when it runs there.
3. **What this costs is said, not hidden:**
   - people in Europe wait longer for each page;
   - their data is stored in the United States, which `/privacy` says,
     from the server;
   - the legal basis for that transfer is a question for the legal review
     of `/privacy` (D-0012 §E, D-0021 §H).

## Alternatives considered

| | Option | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Keep the EU: a new Neon project in Frankfurt | The founder expects most people from outside Europe, and no law requires it. |
| N2 | Washington, D.C. (`iad1`) with a new database in Virginia (us-east-1) | It works as well, but the founder's database already exists in Ohio, and Cleveland is beside it. |

## Cost and risk

A region setting, a place name, a manifest line and the records: under an
hour of agent work, `ESTIMATED`. Reversible: the region is one setting, and
the database is still empty.
