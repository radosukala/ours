# P-0016 — One our.one, signed in or not

**Status:** ADOPTED, by the founder's words of 3 October 2026, as recorded
in [`D-0023`](../decisions/D-0023.md).
**Requests:** D-0023 (CONSTITUTIONAL)
**Proposed by:** the founder
**Drafted by:** an agent, from the founder's words and the two screenshots
**Evidence:** [`P-0016.evidence-conversation-2026-10-03.md`](./P-0016.evidence-conversation-2026-10-03.md)

## The problem

- **The signed-in app is a copy of X.** Its colours are X's own: the blue,
  the pink of a like, the near-black ink. So are its left navigation and
  its black "Post" pill. The founder asked for a familiar feed (D-0011,
  P-0007); the result is X's look, not a familiar one.
- **Signed in, our.one is only the feed.** The front door sends members to
  `/home`, the signed-in pages show none of the network, and the wordmark
  goes to the feed. A member can't see the message, the projects, the
  builders or what is open. M-0017's re-check named this (its RC8) and left
  it for the founder.
- **Two identities.** Every public page has our.one's own look since
  D-0020; the moment a person signs in, it changes.

## What is proposed

1. **One identity, signed in or not:**
   - the app takes the public pages' paper, ink, rust and acid, their
     wordmark and their type;
   - the feed keeps a familiar shape (write, then the posts, newest
     first), in our.one's own look;
   - the pictures of the app on `/feed` follow it.
2. **One header:** members get the public header, with the four places,
   and on its right the feed, notifications and their own pages.
3. **The front door is for members too:**
   - `/` stops sending members to their feed;
   - where a visitor is asked to join, a member is shown their feed.
4. **The feed is named the feed,** and beside it a panel says what our.one
   is, that the feed is its first project, and how to name a need or bring
   an idea.

## Cost and risk

The app's shell and its stylesheet change, and the pages built on them,
with the tests that read them: about a day of agent work, `ESTIMATED`, and
one verification round. Reversible; no data changes.
