## The problem

- **The first screen asks for nothing a stranger can do in ten seconds.**
  After the headline come a paragraph, two buttons that scroll the page, and
  a picture. The form that actually takes a seat is a tab further down.
- **Nobody is told how far from 100,000 it is.** The count and the pledge
  that gives it meaning are a tab down, in the feed's panel.
- **our.one learns nothing from people who aren't ready to join.** A visitor
  who would take back an app has no place to say which one.
- **Shared links show the old site.** X, Facebook and Slack show a card made
  when the domain served another business, and nothing on our.one tells them
  otherwise.

## What is proposed

1. **The first screen, in this order:** the headline, one line ("It starts
   with a friends feed."), the count against 100,000 with the pledge under
   it, the status line, then **one form**: the address, and the optional
   question *"Which app would you take back?"*.
2. **A named app is kept as words and a day, nothing else:** no address, no
   account, no network address, no link to any of them. It is counted, and
   the page says how many; it is read by the founder in the database;
   **nothing publishes it.**
3. **Members** see *"You're in."* in the form's place, and the panel beside
   the feed says how many of 100,000 are in.
4. **A link card:** the headline on the paper, for X, Facebook, Slack and
   anything that reads a page's card.
5. **The records move with it:** `SPEC.md` §18.23, the kit's manifest and
   `/privacy` each say what the new table keeps, and the same thing.

## What it does not propose

- publishing, grouping, ranking or answering the named apps;
- any promise that a named app will be built, or that naming one gives
  anyone a say. The count of named apps is **not authority** (AGENTS.md §5):
  it can't decide what gets built or who decides;
- opening seats. How many to open in `/admin` at launch is the founder's;
- a deploy. Building is not deploying (AGENTS.md §8).

## Open, and the founder's

1. **Whether the named apps are ever published, and how.** Until the founder
   decides, they are read in the database and nothing more.
2. **How many seats to open** when this goes out.
3. **The sentence after "It starts with a friends feed"**, and which
   sentence is the pledge: D-0024 §A chose the shortest true ones.
4. **The two entrance buttons** ("I want this to exist", "I want to build"):
   D-0024 §A removes them from the first screen, since one form is the
   entrance now; the places they led to stay in the header and below.
5. **Whether "1 app named so far." should say "answers".** It counts what
   people typed, not different apps: five people can name one.
