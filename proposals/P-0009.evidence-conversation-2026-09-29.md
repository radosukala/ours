# P-0009 evidence — the founder's words, 29 September 2026

The founder's messages below are verbatim, in the order he sent them. The
agent's answers are summarised after each one. The drafts they point to sit
in `docs-internal/`, which is not published: it is the founder's scratch
space. What this proposal relies on from them is restated in `P-0009.md`.

## 1. The question

> we are building our.one ... have a look at latest version. I’m not sure
> that we will attract people to join with present design and message.
> Have a look. Yes we wanted simple layout and design ... but if I never
> heard of what we try to build and visit a website I’m not sure that it
> will convert me :(

**The agent's answer** (`docs-internal/the-front-door.md`, and a prototype,
`docs-internal/prototype/front-v3.html`, published privately to the founder
as a claude.ai artifact):

- **The verdict:** the simplicity is not the problem. The page answers *who
  owns it and why trust him* before *what is it and why want it*.
- **The walk-through.** The agent took the whole path a stranger takes on
  the local build: the front page, an email, the link, the join form and
  the first screen. It found five problems:
  1. the headline is about the founder, and "it" is never defined;
  2. the product gets one sentence of mechanisms;
  3. "11 people are in", "999 seats open" and "100,000" read as an empty
     room;
  4. the design borrows X's palette and timeline;
  5. nothing warns that the feed starts empty.
- **The proposal:** want, then how, then trust, then act.
  - Headline A: *"Just your people. Then you're done."* (B was *"Remember
    when it was just your friends?"*).
  - The promise as a signed card on the first screen.
  - The 7% Instagram figure from FTC v. Meta.
  - How it works, in three steps.
  - *"Keep your people. Change who runs it."* heading the section that
    holds the count.
  - Product questions first.
- **Test before building:** show five strangers the first screen for five
  seconds, with the bar set in advance.
- **What copy can't fix:**
  - the empty feed after a seat join;
  - the invite page, which is the real front door;
  - posts that are words only.
