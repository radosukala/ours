## The problem

our.one was about to launch as a feed with a promise:

- the founder maintains it;
- at 100,000 accounts the domain, the data and the right to replace the
  maintainer go to a members' body (D-0012).

On 1 October the founder said this leaves the larger opportunity unused.

- **Builders.** Many people try to make a living from apps, with little
  chance. Today's AI coding lets them build far more than before, if people
  gather to want and fund what they build.
- **"We cannot start with feed only."** The message should be a framework
  that anyone can work under, with the feed as its first project.
- **User ownership must be "simply and well defined and understandable".**

## What is proposed

1. **A framework, with the feed as its first project.** our.one becomes a
   network of services:
   - their users fund them;
   - they will control them;
   - maintainers build, improve and run them.

   The feed runs under the same framework. The founder is its maintainer,
   unpaid by declared choice.
2. **A definition of user ownership by decisions:**

   > Owned by its users means: its users, together, decide its essential
   > rules, approve its budget, and can change who runs it while the service
   > keeps going. Nobody can sell that away from them, and each person can
   > always take their own data and leave.

   It defines the word and never claims it of anything today. None of the
   collective rights is in force on our.one yet.
3. **A two-way agreement:**
   - users get control of the important rules, an agreed budget, the right
     to change the operator, and continuity;
   - maintainers get a defined scope, freedom over ordinary product
     decisions, agreed pay once funded, a term and renewal, fair notice,
     a dispute process, and attribution.
4. **Two kinds of project:**
   - **independent experiments,** whose maintainer holds everything. They
     are labelled as such and claim nothing;
   - **protected services,** whose name, domain, data, deploy and funds a
     holder keeps, while the maintainer works with revocable access.

   The test for a protected service is whether an appointed successor can
   continue it without the former maintainer able to block it.
5. **Traction means the first service its users fund.**
   - The holder is set up then. It must be able to act as data controller
     and to hold funds.
   - Each service's schedule names its own route to user control.
   - 100,000 stays the feed's trigger.
6. **Money:**
   - shared costs are public and approved by those who pay them;
   - nobody invests in any service for a return;
   - revenue is not income.
7. **From interest to customers.**
   - Builders propose services, and people name needs.
   - A proposal states the need, what users would have to move, the price,
     the scope and the budget (including pay), and what it asks for now.
   - Interest, a trial and an intention to buy are counted separately. None
     of them is a payment.
8. **The data line,** at the founder's request of 2 October. No maintainer
   can take members' data for their own use or for sale. Data and
   connections are kept centrally by the holder, so that no single app is a
   point of failure.
   - Seven safeguards are proposed: the law (GDPR Art. 28), the agreement,
     no keys, reach, leave, the record, and custody.
   - Each one's status is shown. None is built.
9. **The message, tested before anything changes.**
   - Two lines go to a five-stranger test against today's headline:
     - *"Apps that won't be sold out from under you. The first is a feed of
       just your people. Pitch the next one."*
     - *"We're building our.one so people can bring the software they want
       into existence, and keep control of it."*
   - The public launch carries the framework in readable words, with the
     common agreement marked "being developed".

## What it changes

D-0017 names every adopted record it amends. In short:

- **D-0012 §D:** the word "owned", defined;
- **D-0011:** the v0 ban on third-party applications, for listed
  independent experiments, and invite-only growth, for people naming needs;
- **SHARED-TOOL §9 and THE-LAYER §11:** proposals are open before the first
  community–builder relationship has worked;
- **D-0016:** its held-back builders block, and the front-page text pinned
  by M-0014's tests, once a later decision changes the page.

## Open

These are listed in `D-0017` §K:

- the first independent project and its builder;
- the data line's safeguards;
- whether the holder comes before the first trial;
- whether the feed's identity and connections move to the holder before
  100,000;
- the holder's legal form;
- whether maintainers count as processors (a question for an advokát);
- whether to list independent experiments at all;
- the result of the message test.
