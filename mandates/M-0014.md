## Verification, and when it stops

The rule, declared here before verification begins:

1. **One round,** by two fresh agents in parallel:
   - honesty and the claims scan;
   - rendering and accessibility.
2. **Their test files are committed as written,** before anyone touches
   them. Each defect is a failing test.
3. **Every confirmed finding is fixed or recorded** by the architect. Its
   test is kept and renamed `fixed (…)` or `accepted (…)`.
4. **One fresh re-checker** then looks at the fixes, and at how the
   verifiers' tests were adapted. Its file is committed as written too.
5. **After that, a finding is recorded, not iterated,** unless it is HIGH.
