# M-0021 — The first screen, for people who come from a post

**Status:** ADOPTED, under [`D-0024`](../decisions/D-0024.md).
**Class:** BUILD
**Machine projection:** [`M-0021.yaml`](./M-0021.yaml)

## What it builds

- **The first screen,** in this order: the headline; *"It starts with a
  friends feed: your people, newest first, and then it ends. No ads."*;
  the count against 100,000 with the pledge's sentence; the status line;
  the form. *I want to build* stays as a text link. The sections below are
  unchanged.
- **The count:** `memberCount` against the contract's threshold, as *"1 of
  100,000"*, on the first screen and in the panel beside a member's feed;
  the sentence alone when the count can't be read.
- **The form:** the address, an optional *"Which app would you take
  back?"* of at most 140 characters, and D-0016 §B's button. One submit,
  through the seat request's gates and rate limits; the request goes
  through with or without a need.
- **Needs kept:** a `needs` table (the words, when), in a migration;
  nothing links a need to an address. A public count, *"N apps named so
  far."*, from the first. Kept for 12 months.
- **The privacy notice:** a row for the need: what, why, how long.
- **A link card:** title, description and a committed image in the
  identity, on every public page.
- **`SPEC.md` §18.23.**

## What it does not touch

- `/feed`'s verified words, the contract, the agreement, and what the feed
  does;
- the drafts (D-0020 §E), the seats' rules and the waiting list's;
- the records, the kit, any provider, domain or key; `PROPOSALS_EMAIL`
  and the first wave of seats are the founder's actions.

## Verification

- **Two verifiers** that built none of it: honesty (every new sentence,
  the count's truth in every state, the privacy notice, the needs' denial
  paths: too long, empty, with no seat request, the gates closed), and
  rendering and use (a browser, the first screen at every width, light
  and dark, keyboard, the form's states, the card as crawlers fetch it).
- **Their tests are committed as written,** then the fixes, then one
  re-check, under the stopping rule M-0018 used.
- **The first screen's words go to the founder before the build,** and
  screenshots before anything is pushed.
