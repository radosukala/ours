# M-0021 — The first screen asks one thing

**Status:** ADOPTED, under [`D-0024`](../decisions/D-0024.md). This is the
**second** draft of M-0021: the first, and the build made from it, were lost
unpushed (see
[`P-0017.evidence-conversation-2026-10-05.md`](../proposals/P-0017.evidence-conversation-2026-10-05.md)).
**Class:** BUILD
**Machine projection:** [`M-0021.yaml`](./M-0021.yaml)

## What it builds

- **The first screen,** for a visitor, in the order D-0024 §A sets: the
  headline, one line, *"N of 100,000"* with the pledge under it, the status
  line, then **one form**: the address, and the optional question *"Which
  app would you take back?"*.
- **A named app,** kept in a `needs` table as its words and the day, and
  nothing else, with a migration and its down migration. Counted, shown
  only as a count (*"1 app named so far."*), never published.
- **Members:** *"You're in."* in the form's place; *"N of 100,000 people are
  in."* in the panel beside the feed.
- **A link card** on every public page, with an image of the headline on the
  paper made by `scripts/card.ts`.
- **`SPEC.md` §18.23,** the kit manifest's entry and `/privacy`'s, in the
  same words.

## What it does not touch

- what the feed does, its verified words, and every rule and gate behind
  the pages;
- the records, the kit's tool, any provider, domain or key, the seats an
  administrator opens, and the production database.

## Verification

- **Two verifiers** that built none of it: honesty, and rendering and use
  (a browser, desktop and phone, light and dark, keyboard).
- **Their tests are committed as written,** then the fixes, then one
  re-check, under the stopping rule M-0020 used.
- **Screenshots go to the founder** before anything is pushed.

## The deploy

A push to `main` is a deploy on this project, so building is not enough:
D-0024 and this mandate name **the founder's go, after screenshots,** for it.
The build's receipt says what the founder's instruction of 5 October ("finish
M-0021 and commit to main") was read to cover, and what was done.
