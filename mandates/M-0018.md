# M-0018 — Ready for the first deploy

**Status:** ADOPTED, under [`D-0021`](../decisions/D-0021.md).
**Class:** BUILD
**Machine projection:** [`M-0018.yaml`](./M-0018.yaml)

## What it builds

D-0021 §A's README section, and §C to §E, in `apps/web` and `README.md`.

- **Where the site runs** (§C):
  - the hosting row on `/power`, the hosting and administrator lines on
    `/privacy`, the house rules' line on the administrator, and the notice
    at the top of `/privacy` say what is true on the server that renders
    them;
  - on Vercel's production deployment they name Vercel and its region,
    the database from its address's host with its region, and email as
    today, as stated in this server's configuration;
  - anywhere else they say this copy isn't the deployed site;
  - the administrator lines follow the database.
- **The release** (§D):
  - `apps/web/vercel.json` gives Vercel the build command, the region
    (`fra1`) and the schedule;
  - Vercel's production build of the commit the founder names in
    `OURS_RELEASE` applies the migrations, then creates the founder's
    administrator account from `FOUNDER_EMAIL`, `FOUNDER_HANDLE` and
    `FOUNDER_NAME` if no account exists yet, under the founder script's
    lock;
  - it touches nothing in any other build: another commit, a preview, a
    local build; and it prints only what it did.
- **The weekly email** (§E): the route answers GET as it answers POST,
  scheduled on Mondays at 08:00 UTC.
- **The README:** a first section for visitors who come from our.one.
- **`SPEC.md` §18.20** says all of it.

## What it does not touch

- the founder script's rule that local accounts are fictional;
- the feed's words, the front door's, the contract and the common
  agreement;
- the kit, the rules, the tool and the schema;
- the root `vercel.json`, which builds the page at `oursorg.com`;
- any provider, domain, key or real database: the build is tested against
  a local, fictional database only.

## Acceptance

See [`M-0018.yaml`](./M-0018.yaml). In short:

- **with Vercel's production settings, imitated in a test:** the pages name
  Vercel, Frankfurt, the database and its region, and the email as
  configured; no page says our.one is not deployed; the database's address
  appears on no page;
- **without them:** the pages say this copy isn't the deployed site;
- **the release step:**
  - it does nothing outside the production build of the commit named in
    `OURS_RELEASE`;
  - without a database address it fails the build;
  - it migrates, creates the founder's account once (two runs at once still
    create one), and creates nothing once an account exists;
  - it prints nothing that identifies anyone;
- **the weekly email:** it answers GET and POST alike, and refuses both
  without the secret;
- **the README** says nothing the records don't;
- **the usual checks:** typecheck, lint, tests, the claims scan, a
  production build, and `pnpm ours check M-0018 --changed`.

## Verification

Independent agents that built none of it verify it, as before:

- their tests are committed as written, before any fix;
- every finding is fixed or recorded;
- one re-check follows, then no further round.

They run in clean worktrees only, never in the main checkout, whose
`apps/web/.env.local` may hold real keys (D-0021 §F).
