# M-0017 — The front door

**Status:** ADOPTED, under [`D-0020`](../decisions/D-0020.md).
**Class:** BUILD
**Machine projection:** [`M-0017.yaml`](./M-0017.yaml)

## What it builds

D-0020 §A to §F, in `apps/web`, and the idea step in `kit/build.md`.

- **`/`, the front door:** the message, the two entrances, the feed as the
  first project with the labelled possibilities, the illustration, what
  "ours" means as proposed, the builders' invitation, what's in the open,
  and the ways to take part.
- **`/feed`, the feed's page:** the front page's words, form and promises,
  moved unchanged. Signed-in visitors go to `/home` from `/` and from
  `/feed`, as before.
- **The public header and footer,** and the identity on every public page.
  The signed-in app keeps its look.
- **`/projects`, `/build` and `/maintainers`** as D-0020 §B says, and
  `/privacy`'s sentence on the drafts.
- **The drafts:** a need or an idea, written in the browser, copied, or
  opened in the visitor's own email app when `PROPOSALS_EMAIL` is set.
  Nothing is sent, stored or counted. Without JavaScript, the buttons lead
  to `/maintainers`.
- **`kit/build.md`:** the idea first, in `PITCH.md`, then the person's
  choice to find out first or to build now. `kit/README.md` and the line
  for a coding agent follow.
- **The claims scan** reads every new page and component, as it reads the
  rest of `src/app` and `src/components`.

## What it does not touch

- the feed's verified words, its contract and the common agreement's words;
- the rules, the tool and the schema (D-0019);
- any route for needs or ideas but email, and any stored draft;
- the safeguards, which stay not built;
- the hosting lines on `/privacy` and `/power`, which M-0012's precondition
  11 names;
- any data, schema, provider or domain.

## How it is checked

- **The suite:**
  - the denial paths first: no draft leaves the browser by itself, the
    email button appears only with `PROPOSALS_EMAIL` set, and the joining
    gates on `/feed` are the ones `/` had;
  - every sentence on the new pages against the records and the claims
    scan;
  - the feed's words, form and promises on `/feed`, unchanged;
  - typecheck, lint and a production build;
  - an HTTP check of every public page.
- **In a browser:** desktop and phone widths from 320px, light and dark,
  keyboard only, and without JavaScript.
- **An agent trial:** a fresh agent, given only the new line and a
  FICTIONAL person's answers, against a local production build.
- **Independent verifiers** come next. Their tests are committed as written,
  before any is adapted.
- **One re-check** follows, under a stopping rule declared in advance.

**Nothing is deployed.** Deploying is M-0012's, on the founder's go.
