# M-0020 — One our.one, signed in or not

**Status:** ADOPTED, under [`D-0023`](../decisions/D-0023.md).
**Class:** BUILD
**Machine projection:** [`M-0020.yaml`](./M-0020.yaml)

## What it builds

- **One identity:** our.one's tokens become the whole site's, so every
  signed-in page takes the paper, ink, rust and acid, the wordmark and the
  type; the pictures of the app on `/feed` follow.
- **One header:** the public header on every signed-in page. Its right side
  holds a member's feed, notifications and pages, and on a phone the bottom
  bar stays. No left navigation, no "Post" pill.
- **The front door for members:**
  - `/` and the header show a member *"Your feed"*;
  - where a visitor is asked to join, a member is shown their feed;
  - nothing sends a member away from `/`.
- **The feed as the first project:** "Feed", not "Home", and a panel beside
  it with the line, the first project, and naming a need or bringing an
  idea.
- **`SPEC.md` §18.22.**

## What it does not touch

- what the feed does, its verified words, and every rule and gate behind
  the pages;
- the records, the kit, any provider, domain or key.

## Verification

- **Two verifiers** that built none of it: honesty, and rendering and use
  (a browser, every signed-in page, desktop and phone, light and dark,
  keyboard).
- **Their tests are committed as written,** then the fixes, then one
  re-check, under the stopping rule M-0018 used.
- **Screenshots go to the founder** before anything is pushed.
