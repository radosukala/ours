## What is built

`apps/web` has two parts:

- **The core.** It holds the identity, connections, posts and permissions.
  It is plain TypeScript over Postgres with no framework imports.
- **The mobile-ready web application** on top of the core.

People and connections:

- identity by an emailed link;
- accounts by invitation only, with a limit per person;
- mutual friendship and one-way following;
- blocking and muting.

Posts and the feed:

- text posts, to friends or to friends and followers;
- one level of replies;
- likes, seen only by the author;
- a feed in order that marks where you were caught up and ends at fourteen
  days;
- in-app notifications.

Safety and your data:

- reporting, with a moderation queue and statements of reasons;
- export and deletion;
- one weekly email that says who posted, not what.

Public pages, readable without joining:

- the landing page;
- the rules;
- the privacy notice, whose account creation stays off until a controller
  is named;
- the costs, from a versioned ledger file;
- who controls what, from a versioned control file;
- the counts.

## What is not built

- search, suggestions, contact upload, ranking, advertising, photos;
- third-party applications;
- the iOS and Android apps. Those are later mandates on the same core.

## What is not done

- anything deployed;
- any domain touched;
- any provider account used;
- any email sent to a real address;
- any real person's data held.

The release is a separate mandate after `D-0007`'s conditions that apply to
this application.

## Acceptance

See the projection. In short:

- the denial suite passes against a real Postgres;
- the claims scan passes over every public string;
- typecheck, lint and a production build pass;
- an independent verification by agents that did not build it has run to
  its declared stopping rule, and its findings are fixed or recorded.
