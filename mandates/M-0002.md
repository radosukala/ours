# M-0002 — Record the operator, enforce scope against the working tree

**Status:** TESTED — built and receipted 30 August 2026 while marked DRAFT, status corrected under `D-0004` §C on 8 September 2026
**Class:** BUILD
**Authorizing decision:** [`D-0002`](../decisions/D-0002.md)
**Machine projection:** [`M-0002.yaml`](./M-0002.yaml)

## Objective

Record the transitional operator's stated entity facts in the founding
authority, marked unverified, and wire the scope check to the working tree so
a mandate's declared bounds are evaluated against what actually changed rather
than against nothing.

## Human outcome

A reader can see who the transitional operator is and how far that is
evidenced. And an implementation that writes outside its mandate is refused,
rather than reported as coherent because nobody asked the question.

## Part one — what is recorded, and what is not

Recorded as **stated by the founder on 30 August 2026, not verified**:

```text
ENTITY              Ctrl AI, Inc.
FORM                Delaware C-Corporation
REGISTERED OFFICE   800 North King Street, Wilmington, DE 19801
```

Still `[CONFIRM]`, and not to be inferred from the above:

- the Delaware file number and date of incorporation;
- officers, directors, and ownership;
- the founder's legal relationship to the entity;
- whether it holds, or will hold, any asset of this project.

A Delaware registered office is a service address, not a place of business.
It establishes the state of incorporation and where process is served. It does
not establish where the company operates or who controls it, and the record
must not read as though it does.

## Part two — the failure being corrected

While building `M-0001`, the implementation wrote
`receipts/builds/2026-08-30-M-0001.md`. `M-0001` denies `receipts/**`.

**The mandate contradicted itself.** It required a build receipt in its
evidence section and denied the only directory one could be written to. That
guarantees either a violation or a missing receipt. This mandate therefore
allows `receipts/builds/**`, and every future mandate requiring a receipt must
do the same.

**Nothing objected.** `R-SCOPE` evaluates `changedPaths`, and no caller ever
supplied any. The check reported, accurately, *"Scope is coherent. No changed
paths were supplied, so nothing was checked against it"* — and that was read
as a pass.

The article was `ENFORCED` in the kernel and `DECLARED` in practice. That gap
is the single most important thing found so far, because it is the shape of
every future failure here: a check that exists, runs, reports truthfully, and
decides nothing, while a reader counts it as protection.

## Fixes

1. `ours check` accepts `--changed`, reading `git diff --name-only` plus
   untracked files, and evaluates scope against them.
2. When no paths are supplied, `R-SCOPE` reports `NOT_MACHINE_DECIDABLE` with
   the reason, never `PASS`. An unrun check must never read as a clean one.
3. `receipts/builds/**` is allowed wherever a build receipt is required.

## Acceptance tests

1. The founding authority records the entity facts and marks them unverified.
2. No Ctrl AI fact appears anywhere as confirmed.
3. `ours check --changed` reads paths from the working tree.
4. A write outside the allowed scope is refused when paths are supplied.
5. An unsupplied scope check reports `NOT_MACHINE_DECIDABLE`, not `PASS`.
6. `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` pass.

## Stop conditions

- recording a fact would require inventing or inferring it;
- an acceptance test would require weakening a constitutional article.

## Rollback
