# M-0013 — The front page, product first

**Status:** ADOPTED under [`D-0015`](../decisions/D-0015.md) §I, by the
founder's instruction of 29 September 2026: *"yes to your prototype, this is
much better homepage! Please build it"*.
**Class:** BUILD. **Nothing is deployed** under this mandate.
**Machine projection:** [`M-0013.yaml`](./M-0013.yaml)

## What it builds

All of it is in `apps/web`, on the signed-out front page `/`:

1. **The first screen.**
   - The headline *"Just your people. Then you're done."*
   - The lede, and Get in.
   - The founder's promise as a signed card.
   - A phone whose feed ends at *You're caught up*.
2. **"Where did your friends go?"**
   - The court's 7% finding, linked to its source.
   - An illustration of a ranked feed beside one that ends.
3. **How it works,** in three steps.
4. **"Keep your people. Change who runs it."**
   - WhatsApp in three dated lines, and the owner and maintainer line.
   - The handover promise and what holds it today.
   - The count, as a rank.
5. **Fair questions,** product ones first.
6. **The claims scan.**
   - Its handover rule catches "hand it to".
   - The new sentence is listed by exact text.

The exact copy is in `apps/web/SPEC.md` §18.15.

## What it does not change

- **The answer after Get in** (D-0015 §H).
- **The invite page, and every other page.**
- **The seats and the waiting list,** except where the front page shows
  their line.
- **The schema and the data.** There is no migration.

## Constraints and acceptance

These are in the YAML projection.

**The two that matter most:**

- **Every sentence about the handover is listed by exact text,** and none
  says it has happened.
- **Every number has a source,** and the 7% sentence waits for a human's
  confirmation before it is published.
