# M-0012 — Deploy our.one

**Status:** DRAFT, under [`D-0013`](../decisions/D-0013.md) §G, amended by
[`D-0014`](../decisions/D-0014.md) §D. It is adopted by the founder's
explicit go in chat, once its preconditions hold. Until then, nothing is
deployed.
**Class:** DEPLOY
**Machine projection:** [`M-0012.yaml`](./M-0012.yaml)

## What it does

It publishes the build verified under M-0011 as `our.one`:

- Vercel, with Neon as the database and Resend for email;
- the database migrated;
- the founder's own account created as the one administrator;
- a proof over HTTP that what runs is what was verified.

Seats stay closed until the founder opens them.

## What the founder does first

No agent handles a secret, so these steps are the founder's:

1. **The written designation** of Ctrl AI's representative in the EU, signed
   (GDPR Article 27(1); D-0014 §B). D-0014 settles the contact email
   (`privacy@ctrlai.com`) and the choice of a representative.
2. **Vercel:** a project linked to `github.com/radosukala/ours`, with root
   directory `apps/web`.
3. **Neon:** a database in an EU region. Its connection URL goes into
   Vercel as `DATABASE_URL`.
4. **Resend:** the `our.one` domain verified, by adding its DNS records.
5. **In Vercel's environment settings,** enter:
   - `SESSION_SECRET` and `CRON_SECRET`;
   - `RESEND_API_KEY`, `MAIL_FROM` and `MAIL_TRANSPORT=resend`;
   - `DATA_CONTROLLER=Ctrl AI, Inc.`,
     `DATA_CONTROLLER_EMAIL=privacy@ctrlai.com` and
     `DATA_CONTROLLER_REPRESENTATIVE=Rado Sukala`;
   - `CLIENT_IP_HEADER=x-vercel-forwarded-for`;
   - `APP_URL=https://our.one`.
6. **The domain:** move ONE to `oneburger.cz`, then point `our.one` at
   Vercel.

## Then, on the founder's go

The agent:

- runs the migrations;
- creates the founder's administrator account (the one real address);
- checks every page and the health endpoint over HTTP;
- takes screenshots;
- writes the release receipt.

The founder then opens seats when the launch starts.
