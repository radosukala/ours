# M-0016 — The build kit

**Status:** ADOPTED, under [`D-0019`](../decisions/D-0019.md).
**Class:** BUILD
**Machine projection:** [`M-0016.yaml`](./M-0016.yaml)

## What it builds

D-0019 §B to §G.

- **`kit/`** in the repository:
  - `build.md`, the instructions a coding agent follows;
  - `our-one.mjs`, with `init`, `check` and `rules`;
  - `our.one.schema.json`;
  - a README for people who browse the repository.
- **The feed's own `our.one.json` and rules block,** in `apps/web`. The feed
  passes the check, and a test keeps it passing.
- **`/build`, "Build on our.one",** and the routes that serve the kit from
  the deployed commit: `/build.md`, `/kit/our-one.mjs` and
  `/kit/our.one.schema.json`.
- **The footer's "Build with us"** goes to `/build`. `/maintainers` and
  `/projects` link to it, and `/projects` says the feed passes the check.
- **The claims scan reads the kit's text.**

## What it does not touch

- the seven safeguards, which stay not built;
- any registry, listing or marketplace;
- any route for proposals but email;
- the front page's headline and lede;
- the common agreement's words and the contract's promises;
- any data, schema or provider.

## How it is checked

- **The suite:**
  - the denial paths first: every rule fails on a project that breaks it,
    and the tool makes no network request, writes only what `init` names,
    and never prints a secret it finds;
  - the feed passes;
  - the pages' copy, the served files and their hashes;
  - the claims scan, typecheck, lint and a production build;
  - an HTTP check.
- **Independent verifiers** come next. Their tests are committed as written,
  before any is adapted.
- **One re-check** follows, under a stopping rule declared in advance.

**Nothing is deployed.** Deploying is M-0012's, on the founder's go.
