# M-0004 — Adoption as an event, the checks that hold it, cost as a column, and the receipt still owed

**Status:** ADOPTED — by the founder on 8 September 2026, under `D-0004`, in the same adopting commit as `D-0006`
**Class:** BUILD
**Authorizing decision:** [`D-0004`](../decisions/D-0004.md) §D
**Machine projection:** [`M-0004.yaml`](./M-0004.yaml)
**Drafted:** 8 September 2026 — because a colleague's review of `M-0006`
found that its acceptance rested on a check that cannot tell a draft
record from an adopted one, which `D-0004` had already named as a gap.
This mandate closes it, and everything after it depends on that.

## Objective

Make adoption an event the kernel can see, and a prerequisite it can
refuse on. Four pieces of kernel work, two of record-keeping, and one debt:

1. **The adoption check.** A mandate is refused while its own status, its
   decision's status, or the founding authority's status is `DRAFT`.
   Reported as `CHECKED` first; `ENFORCED` once the founder's reconciliation
   in `D-0004` §C is committed, so that the six records acted on while
   `DRAFT` do not refuse themselves before they are corrected.
2. **The prerequisite check.** A decision or mandate may name
   `prerequisite_decisions`; the kernel refuses while any named decision
   is missing or not `ADOPTED`. `ENFORCED`.
3. **The report tells the two apart.** `ours check` ends in one of two
   lines — *VALID AS A DRAFT — not authorised for execution*, naming the
   `DRAFT` record, or *AUTHORISED FOR EXECUTION* — and never a bare
   `AUTHORISED` that could mean either.
4. **Denial coverage.** Tests for: an `ADOPTED` mandate under a `DRAFT`
   decision; a `DRAFT` mandate under an `ADOPTED` decision; a `DRAFT`
   founding authority; a missing prerequisite decision; a prerequisite
   decision still `DRAFT` — each asserting the article and the message.
5. **Cost as a column.** The fields of `LIFECYCLE.md` §4 in the schemas and
   receipts; earlier receipts back-filled from the provider's usage record
   where it exists, marked `ESTIMATED` where it does not, left blank rather
   than invented.
6. **The layout.** `README.md` lists `proposals/` and `envelopes/`; the
   `our.one` row enters the asset map in `authority/FOUNDING-AUTHORITY.md`
   §4 with the text `D-0004` §A gives it.
7. **The receipt still owed.** `receipts/releases/2026-08-30-M-0001-RELEASE.md`
   from verifiable facts: the served page's digest, fetched read-only from
   `oursorg.com` and hashed; the deploying commit; the environment; the
   deploy authority; the rollback target; and the residue `M-0001-RELEASE`
   declared. If the served digest cannot be matched to a commit's build,
   the receipt says so and `M-0001-RELEASE` stays `ADOPTED`, not `DEPLOYED`.

## Human outcome

A reader who runs one command can tell whether a mandate is merely
well-formed or actually authorised to be acted on; a record acted on while
`DRAFT` can no longer pass unnoticed; and every change carries the cost of
the judgment, the verification, and the code beside each other.

## Adopted constraints

- No status of any record changes under this mandate. Status flips are the
  founder's, in `D-0004`'s adopting commit; this mandate builds the check
  that would have caught their absence.
- The adoption check is `CHECKED` until the reconciliation is committed and
  `ENFORCED` after; the receipt records the commit at which it switched.
- No fact marked `[CONFIRM]` is resolved. The release receipt records what
  can be verified and marks the rest.
- No path outside the allowed scope; the one row in the founding authority
  is the only text this mandate may add there, and only with `D-0004` §A's
  words.

## Hypotheses

1. The six reconciled records validate under the `ENFORCED` check without
   any change beyond their status fields.
2. The served digest at `oursorg.com` matches a build from the deploying
   commit.

## Acceptance tests

1. `pnpm ours check M-0004` is *authorised for execution* once `D-0004`,
   `M-0004`, and the founding authority are `ADOPTED` — and *valid as a
   draft* before.
2. Each denial in objective 4 refuses, asserting the article and the
   message.
3. Every mandate in `mandates/` reports one of the two result lines and
   never a bare `AUTHORISED`.
4. `M-0000`–`M-0002` validate under the `ENFORCED` check after
   reconciliation, with no change beyond status fields.
5. The cost fields are present on every receipt, each with a source or
   `ESTIMATED`, none invented.
6. The release receipt names the served digest, the commit, the environment,
   the authority, the rollback target, and the residue — or states which of
   those could not be verified.
7. `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm test` pass; `pnpm ours check M-0004 --changed`
   authorises the finished working tree.

## Evidence required

- test output naming each denial and its reason;
- the release receipt;
- a build receipt recording agent, model, scope touched, cost, and every
  failure.

## Stop conditions

- a check would require changing any record's status;
- the served digest cannot be fetched, or fetching it would require more
  than a read;
- a cost figure would have to be invented;
- the work requires a path outside the allowed scope.

## Rollback

Revert the kernel, CLI, verifier, schema, and test changes; remove the
release receipt and the back-filled fields. Reversible. **Irreversible
residue: none.**
