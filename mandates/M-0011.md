# M-0011 — The launch website

**Status:** ADOPTED under [`D-0012`](../decisions/D-0012.md) §F, by the
founder's instruction of 28 September 2026: *"What we need now is the
MESSAGE that will resonate, build website, launched."*
**Class:** BUILD. **Nothing is deployed** under this mandate.
**Machine projection:** [`M-0011.yaml`](./M-0011.yaml)

## What it builds

All of it is in `apps/web`:

1. **The front page.**
   - The message of `D-0012` §D: *"Today it's mine. At [threshold]
     members, I give it away."*
   - The live count, shown as a rank: *"N people are in. You'd be #N+1."*
   - The threshold is one constant, 100,000 until the founder confirms the
     number.
   - The contract in brief, and the story.
   - The answers to a skeptic, including what happens if the threshold is
     never reached.
2. **`/contract`.** The maintainer's promises, each with what holds it
   today, and the handover promise with its conditions.
3. **Seats and a waiting list** for people without an invite. Both stay
   off until a controller is named.
   - **Seats.** An administrator opens a number of them, say 1,000 on
     launch day. A visitor who takes one gets the join link at once, with
     the maintainer recorded as the inviter.
   - **The waiting list.** With no seats open, the address waits in line.
     Opening seats invites the oldest addresses first.
   - The list keeps only an address and a time. An address is deleted when
     it is invited, or when its owner asks.
4. **The name.** our.one wherever a person reads it. OURS stays in code
   comments and the repository.
5. **The status line:** *"Maintained by its founder. Handed to its members
   at [threshold]."*
6. **The weekly email** names the most recent posters, not the most active.
7. **The claims scan.** It permits only the listed sentences of the
   handover promise, by exact text.

## Constraints and acceptance

These are in the YAML projection.

**The two that matter most:**

- **No page may say the handover has happened.**
- **The count counts only real accounts,** never the waiting list.

## Release

**A later release mandate** deploys this, after `D-0012` §E's conditions
are met:

- the named controller;
- the legal review;
- the accounts.
