# M-0015 — The framework pages

**Status:** ADOPTED, under [`D-0018`](../decisions/D-0018.md).
**Class:** BUILD
**Machine projection:** [`M-0015.yaml`](./M-0015.yaml)

## What it builds

D-0018 §B to §E, in `apps/web`. The copy is `apps/web/SPEC.md` §18.17, word
for word.

- **`/agreement`:** the common agreement in readable words, marked *"being
  developed"*.
  - Every part says what holds it today.
  - Each of the seven data safeguards is marked not built.
- **`/projects`:**
  - the feed as the first project: its lede, its maintainer, no pay by
    choice, its budget, what the founder holds today, the promise at the
    threshold, and its exception;
  - an invitation to propose the next one.
- **`/maintainers`, "Build the next one":**
  - what a maintainer does;
  - what they get and what they give up;
  - how to propose a service;
  - how anyone can name a need.

  Proposals and needs go by email to `PROPOSALS_EMAIL` and are read by hand.
  While the setting is empty, the page says proposals open at launch.
- **The footer** links to all three, on every page.
- **`/contract`** says the feed will also run under the agreement.
- **`/privacy`** describes emailed proposals, but only while the setting is
  set.
- **The claims scan** lets three exact sentences through on `/agreement`
  only:
  - the definition of "owned";
  - the sentence that applies it;
  - the agreement's denial of investment.

## What it does not touch

- the front page's headline and lede, which wait for the message test;
- the contract's eight promises;
- any data, schema or provider;
- any form or stored record for proposals.

## How it is checked

- **The suite:**
  - tests for every acceptance line;
  - the claims scan;
  - typecheck, lint and a production build;
  - an HTTP check of the three pages.
- **Independent verifiers** come next. Their tests are committed as written,
  before any is adapted.
- **One re-check** follows, under a stopping rule declared in advance.

**Nothing is deployed.** Deploying is M-0012's, on the founder's go.
