# D-4 — Say what "a majority of those who respond" counts, and re-pin — FICTIONAL

**Status:** ADOPTED — by 18 of 23 members on 5 September 2026, vote `V-4` · **a sample record; nobody here exists**
**Class:** CHARTER — an amendment to the founding arrangement
**Authority:** the members, by two thirds — `C-DECISIONS`
**Machine projection:** [`D-4.yaml`](./D-4.yaml)

## The decision

Amend `C-DECISIONS` of the founding arrangement so that its words match the
procedure the kernel counts: rules and improvements are decided by *a
majority of those who vote for or against*, and an abstention is recorded
and does not decide. Re-pin the charter and the booking specification under
this decision.

## Why

The arrangement said "a majority of those who respond". The kernel's
procedure `majority-of-respondents` counts those who vote for or against,
so a vote of seven for, none against, and eight abstaining passed as a
majority while a reader of the arrangement might have counted the
abstentions as responses. The words and the count must agree, and the
members chose the count: an abstention is a recorded refusal to decide, not
a vote against.

## What this decision authorizes

Nothing to build. It amends the arrangement and pins the current files.

## Dissent

One against — abstentions should count as responses, so that a small
minority cannot decide when most members abstain. Preserved in `V-4`; the
members answered that the quorum question belongs to a later amendment and
is not settled here.

## Appeal

None outside the group — recorded as a gap.
